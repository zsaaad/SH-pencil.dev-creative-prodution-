## Design System Tokens

```
Colors:
  Brand orange:   #ff9419
  Brand dark:     #2f2922
  Off-white:      #faf7f2
  Paper:          #f5f5f0
  Near-black:     #0d0d0d / #0a0a0a
  Deep dark:      #1a1208
  Red (loss):     #e53e3e
  Warm yellow:    #fff8e1

Typography:
  Headlines:  Barlow, weight 900 (Black), 60–210px
  Sub-heads:  Open Sans or Barlow Bold, 30–52px
  Body:       Open Sans Regular, 28–38px
  Labels:     Open Sans, 22–24px, all-caps, letter-spaced

Spacing:
  Frame padding:  64px (all sides)
  Section gap:    20–32px
  Divider:        80×4px rect, #ff9419

CTA Button:
  Fill:         #ff9419 (or #2f2922 on orange bg)
  Text:         Barlow Bold 28–32px, white
  Border-radius: 8px
  Padding:      20px uniform

Logo sizing:
  Width: 200–220px, Height: 40–44px
  Dark bg → white reverse logo
  Light bg → orange logo
  Orange bg → white reverse logo
```
