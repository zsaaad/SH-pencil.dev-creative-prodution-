## Global brief recap (binding)

- **Brand colours (hex only):** `#ff9419` orange · `#2f2922` black · `#ff630f` bold orange · `#ff546f` pink · `#2a6ee8` azure · `#fff8ea` cream · `#ffffff` white. Per-frame off-palette accents are allowed only when the brief explicitly calls for them (e.g. `#FF398C`, `#D7F35B`, `#FFD93A`, `#E63946`).
- **Type:** Headline = Barlow Black · Sub = Open Sans Semibold · Body = Open Sans Regular · CTA = Open Sans Bold ALL CAPS
- **CTA verbatim, every frame:** `BOOK A FREE DEMO NOW` on orange `#ff9419` pill, white text
- **Logo:** StoreHub wordmark bottom-left (white on dark/orange, full-colour on light)
- **Product anchor:** From RM3.40/day · 24-hour setup · 17,000+ MY merchants
- **Family principle:** Illustration + character-driven. Pan-Asian characters illustrated in a consistent style — friendly, confident, "ownership-of-shop" energy.
- **Locked illustration style for Family I (binding across all 12 frames):** Flat vector / mid-century-modern illustration, bold outlines (3px), warm flat colour fills (orange `#ff9419`, black `#2f2922`, cream `#fff8ea`, pink `#ff546f`, plus the per-frame accent bg). Soft hand-drawn imperfections; no gradients, no 3D, no photorealism. Characters: Pan-Asian (East/Southeast Asian), aprons/uniforms appropriate to F&B/retail, expressive but not cartoonish. Faces shown 3/4 angle, friendly half-smile, eyes visible. Same character-design rules across all 12.
- **Hard exclusions:** No delivery commission framing · No Beep · No Western faces · No real competitor names · No real third-party logos · No unverified claims
