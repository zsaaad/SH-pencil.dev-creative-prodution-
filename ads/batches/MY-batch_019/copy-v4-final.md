## What changed vs v3, and why

| Slot | v3 (rejected) | v4 |
|---|---|---|
| PLAY | "Run a full order on a real POS. We're timing you." | "Your new hire can run the counter on their first shift." — sells minimal staff training, not a timer game |
| SIM | "Type your shop's name. Watch this page become your shop." | "One system runs your counter, your QR menu and your reports." — sells consolidation |
| DAY | "Scroll and the sun moves. 7am open to 11pm close." | "See every hour your shop trades without standing behind the counter." — a 16-hour day is the merchant's pain, not our value; the value is live visibility away from the counter |
| CALC | "Move three sliders. See the ringgit that never got recorded." | "The sales nobody recorded are the ones costing you." — the pain is the merchant's, not the slider's |
| RCPT | "Scroll, and this page prints itself line by line." | "Every sale recorded and e-Invoicing compliant before you lock up." — sells compliance + closing |
| HB | "A map of Malaysia pinging every time a StoreHub shop sells." | "20,000+ merchants across Southeast Asia run on StoreHub." — the proof is the message |

Kept from v3: subcopy stays a short "From X → Y" transition; CTA labels stay campaign-locked
to the LP verb (the click promise still matches the page); V3 giants stay one strong string.

Features referenced use official names only: Point of Sale (POS), QR Order & Pay,
Inventory Management, Reporting & Analytics, E-Invoice — drawn from the two adjacent
"Seamless Checkouts & Payments" / "Run your store smoothly" groups.
