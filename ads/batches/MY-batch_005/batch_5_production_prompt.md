- **CTA verbatim, every frame:** `BOOK A FREE DEMO NOW` on orange `#ff9419` pill, white text
- **Logo:** StoreHub wordmark bottom-left (white on dark/orange, full-colour on light)
- **Product anchor:** From RM3.40/day · 24-hour setup · 17,000+ MY merchants
- **Imagery:** Pan-Asian merchants only. Documentary feel. 40–60 blur on photo bgs where text overlays; 70–90% opacity colour panel behind text on busy areas.

**Family C pattern:** ONE big specific number is the hero. Everything else supports it. The number must be the biggest visual element on the canvas.
