## Global brief recap (binding for every frame)

- **Brand colours (hex only):** `#ff9419` orange · `#2f2922` black · `#ff630f` bold orange · `#ff546f` pink · `#2a6ee8` azure · `#fff8ea` cream · `#ffffff` white
- **Type:** Headline = Barlow Black · Sub = Open Sans Semibold · Body = Open Sans Regular · CTA = Open Sans Bold ALL CAPS
- **CTA verbatim, every frame:** `BOOK A FREE DEMO NOW` on orange `#ff9419` pill, white text (unless prompt overrides explicitly)
- **Logo:** StoreHub wordmark bottom-left (white on dark/orange, full-colour on light)
- **Product anchor:** From RM3.40/day · 24-hour setup · 17,000+ MY merchants
- **Imagery:** Pan-Asian merchants only where photographic. Artifacts (UI screens, chat threads, calendar grids, browser tabs) are stylised mockups — never use real brand logos or real product names.
- **Hard exclusions:** No delivery commission framing · No Beep · No Western faces · No real competitor names · No real third-party logos (no WhatsApp, no Google, no LHDN seal, no Outlook) — use stylised stand-ins · No unverified claims

**Family D pattern:** The hero is a STYLISED FAKE ARTIFACT that mimics a familiar productivity surface (browser tab, chat thread, calendar grid, search dropdown, OS dialog, etc.) The artifact must look intentionally recognisable as the tool it references, but with a stylised twist that makes it clearly an illustration, not a screenshot. Headline lives in a strip below the artifact, not over it.
