## Family J intent (binding)

Family J creatives must read as **useful free content first, ad second**. Listicles, tip cards, "5 stages of X", "8 ways to cut", "free playbook", "free webinar", "free calculator". The frame itself should look like a tearsheet a merchant would screenshot. The CTA stays visible — never bury it — but the dominant visual is the educational artefact (the list, the chart, the checklist, the calculator UI).

If the content doesn't actually deliver a useful idea in a thumbnail glance, the ad fails. No bait.
