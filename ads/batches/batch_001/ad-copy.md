## Copy Notes

**Character counts are optimised for Meta's display rules:**
- Primary Text: all under 200 chars (125 visible before truncation — key hook is always in the first line)
- Headline: all under 40 chars
- Description: all under 30 chars

**CTA button in Meta Ads Manager:** Use "Book Now" or "Learn More" — the actual CTA text ("BOOK A FREE DEMO NOW") is baked into the creative image.

**Language note:** This is the MY-EN batch. MS and CN versions should be localised, not directly translated — adapt tone, food references, and cultural hooks per language.
