## Family K intent (binding)

Family K is StoreHub's **unfair MY advantage** — pure local, pure cultural pride. None of Adfolio's source brands can replicate this. Settings are kopitiam, mamak, pasar malam, banana-leaf nasi lemak, teh tarik, DuitNow QR, Hari Raya / CNY / Deepavali tables.

**Language rules:**
- Use Bahasa Melayu and Manglish phrases only where they read native — `Selamat Hari Raya`, `Gong Xi`, `RM3.40/hari`, `Apa khabar`, `Boss`, `Lah`
- When in doubt → default to English
- Spell-check every BM word in your head before locking. Examples to nail: `Selamat`, `Hari Raya`, `kedai`, `hari` (not "hri"), `Tutup`, `Gong Xi Fa Cai`
- No tropes. No stereotype lensing. The ad is for merchants, not tourists.
- When showing signage with text inside an AI-generated image: avoid putting BM words inside the image itself; keep BM in the rendered Pencil typography only. AI image gen mangles text.
