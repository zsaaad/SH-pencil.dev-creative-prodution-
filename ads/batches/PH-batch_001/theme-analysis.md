**Structural read-throughs:**
1. **₱63/day price anchor is the single most scalable PH message** — appears in 6 of the top 8 ads and survives multiple relaunches.
2. **Holiday-tied promos work when they carry a real discount anchor (₱21,000 off, ₱63), die when they carry only emotion (NationalHeroesDay, Halloween).**
3. **F&B-specific question hooks ("How much is your profit?") drive the cheapest leads** but lower Won% — they fill the top of funnel and need stronger qualification copy.
4. **Pure testimonial/quote ads underperform** (productcentrictestimonial Won% 1.02%); merchant logo grids work; talking heads do not.
5. **Choose Your Fighter / unboxing visual formats** drive 4–6% CTR — 5–7x the period average — proving comparison and product-as-hero formats are the engagement workhorses.

## 3. The 8 Themes — Ranked

### #1 · T4 The Math · SAFE
**Why PH:** ₱63/day anchor is the most scalable, repeatable PH message proven across 2 quarters and survives every relaunch. Cannot launch a fresh PH batch without it.
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *₱63 hero* — full-bleed price + hardware composition (the proven safe).
2. *₱63 vs jeepney fare* — ₱63 = one jeepney ride home. Localised cost-of-living anchor.
3. *Daily-coin stack* — physical pile of ₱63 coins next to a hardware bundle.
4. *Year-math receipt* — ₱63/day × 365 = ₱22,995 vs commission losses (sets up T9 sibling).

**Localisation:** jeepney fare, sari-sari sachet pricing, palengke daily change.

### #2 · T11 Artifact Native · WILDCARD (highest structural bet)
**Why PH:** MY Batch 1 proved the artifact format drove the best CPSQL (RM358 vs RM425 PH avg). PH has never tested it. PH ad-skepticism is high → artifact disguise should outperform polished promos.
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *Fake JobStreet PH posting* — "HIRING: Cashier + inventory clerk + delivery dispatcher + reports admin. Salary: ₱28,000/mo. OR: StoreHub from ₱63/day."
2. *Fake GCash receipt* — month-end statement showing GrabFood commission, FoodPanda commission, manual overtime; last line "StoreHub: ₱1,890/mo".
3. *Fake Viber boss-to-staff chat* at 11pm reconciling sales — ends "...lipat na tayo sa StoreHub."
4. *Fake BIR-form checkbox* — "Is your POS BIR-accredited? [ ] Yes [ ] No — find out in 30 secs." (ties to Philippine BIR compliance pain — a real PH-only artifact MY can't replicate).

**Localisation:** JobStreet PH, GCash, Viber (PH chat default), BIR form, palengke price-tag handwriting.

### #3 · T5 Competitive Contrast · SAFE
**Why PH:** Choose Your Fighter delivered CTR 4.87–6.76% in PH — the top-engagement format in the dataset. Theme has unique scalability headroom because comparison framing extends naturally beyond hardware tiers.
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *CYF hardware tier ad — refreshed* (the proven safe control).
2. *GrabFood 30% commission vs StoreHub 0%* — two numbers, full stop.
3. *Manual stock count at midnight vs StoreHub dashboard at 6pm* — split panel.
4. *Pen-and-paper karinderya logbook vs StoreHub iPad* — analog-to-digital comparison.

**Localisation:** karinderya logbook, palengke handwritten receipts, sari-sari notebook.

### #4 · T9 Hidden Cost · WILDCARD
**Why PH:** "How much is your profit?" coffee/halohalo/sisig ads drove CPL ₱9–₱41 — the cheapest leads in the entire dataset. The forensic-cost angle is directionally validated; needs sharper artifact-style execution to lift Won%.
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *Annual commission audit receipt* — "GrabFood ₱60k + FoodPanda ₱40k + manual reports 240 hrs = ₱X. StoreHub: ₱22,995/yr."
2. *Sticky note on POS screen* — "23 hours this month doing reports by hand." Subline: StoreHub automates this.
3. *Coffee shop P&L breakdown* — extends winning howmuchisyourprofit_coffee into a fuller cost table.
4. *Karinderya cash-drawer leak* — daily under-ring losses tallied into a month figure.

**Localisation:** karinderya, coffee, halo-halo, sisig, lechon stall — re-use proven food anchors.

### #5 · T2 New Chapter · SAFE
**Why PH:** Independenceday_P21000_promo delivered SQL% **43.1%** and Won% 5.88% — the highest-quality leads in the dataset. The opening/milestone moment converts when paired with a hard offer. Holiday-trigger ads without offers die (NationalHeroesDay, Halloween).
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *"Just opened? POS is the last thing on your list — make it the cheapest."* + ₱21k off hardware visual.
2. *Renovation invoice* with POS line item highlighted as the smallest cost.
3. *Empty-restaurant-pre-opening hero shot* — owner standing at the counter, signage visible, one line: "Open with the #1 POS."
4. *Grand-opening countdown* — checklist style, POS as final ticked item.

**Localisation:** Filipino restaurant fiesta-opening rituals, ribbon-cutting, lechon centrepiece for grand-opening shoot.

### #6 · T12 Milestone Math · WILDCARD
**Why PH:** Combines two proven PH levers — milestone framing (Independence Day SQL% 43%) + ₱63 price anchor — into one creative. Untested fusion. Hypothesis: pre-qualifies for high SQL%.
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *Week-1-of-business itemised receipt* — Renovation ₱200k | Kitchen ₱150k | Signage ₱25k | POS from ₱63/day.
2. *90-day survival math* — "First 90 days decide the next 10 years. ₱63/day buys you the system. The rest is on you."
3. *Year-2 anniversary math* — "Year 1 with GrabFood at 30% = ₱400k gone. Year 2 with StoreHub = ₱22,995."
4. *Opening expansion math* — "Location #2 in 6 months instead of 14 — same dashboard, two stores."

**Localisation:** Filipino family-business multi-generation framing; sari-sari → carinderia → restaurant growth ladder.

### #7 · T7 Cultural Pride · SAFE
**Why PH:** Halo-halo, sisig, coffee, jeepney — the proven engagement triggers in the secondary CSV. Theme has not been tested as a pure cultural-identity hero (food-as-full-bleed-canvas). MY Batch 1 winner CPL was T7. Localised PH version is high-probability scale bet.
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *Lechon hero* — full-bleed lechon, minimal brand chrome, line: "Built for Filipino F&B."
2. *Jeepney as POS metaphor* — "Old reliable runs the country. Now run yours on something modern."
3. *Sari-sari → restaurant ladder* — three-panel evolution of a Filipino business archetype, StoreHub as the constant.
4. *Halo-halo / palengke morning rush* — documentary-style, captures Filipino F&B identity, POS visible but understated.

**Localisation:** lechon, halo-halo, jeepney, sari-sari, palengke, kainan, fiesta. Use real photography — no stock pan-Asian.

### #8 · T6 Social Proof (logo grid only, no quotes) · SAFE
**Why PH:** Logo-grid ads (`allinonepos_fnb_logos_static_relaunch`) hit CPL RM40, Won% 5.77%. Quote-card testimonials died (Won% 1.02%). The format split is the insight — logos work, quotes don't.
**Concept slots (4):**
1. *Wall of PH F&B merchant logos* — density as proof, headline "300+ PH F&B brands run StoreHub."
2. *Map of Metro Manila + Cebu + Davao* with merchant pins — geographic proof.
3. *Specific outcome card (no face, no quote)* — "Binq PH cut closing time 2hrs → 20 mins."
4. *20,000 merchants. 3 countries. 1 POS.* — scale-as-proof minimal layout.

**Localisation:** real PH merchant brand logos; Manila/Cebu/Davao geo split.

## 4. Themes NOT Picked

- **T1 Pain Amplification** — `StressToSuccess` killed it (0 SQL, transformation arc fails in PH). The one survivor (`no1possystemsimple`) is closer to a positioning claim than pain — covered inside T5.
- **T3 Quiet Ambition** — Untested in PH, fresh-market needs proven scaffolding first; revisit Batch 2.
- **T8 Aspirational Self** — Deprioritised globally (1 lead, 0 SQL in MY Batch 1); no PH signal to override.
- **T10 Value Unlocked** — Unboxing-format ads close poorly in PH (CPWon RM13,886 on relaunch). The product-tour angle survives inside T5 (CYF format), not as its own theme.

## 5. Safe / Wildcard Split — **5 Safe : 3 Wildcard**

**Safe (5):** T4 The Math · T5 Competitive Contrast · T2 New Chapter · T7 Cultural Pride · T6 Social Proof (logo-grid only)
**Wildcard (3):** T11 Artifact Native · T9 Hidden Cost · T12 Milestone Math

**Argument:** Fresh-market PH default should weight safe heavier than MY Batch 2's 6/6 split because we have no in-market control to beat — we are setting the baseline, not iterating against one. The 5 safe themes lock in what cross-CSV evidence already proves works (price anchor, comparison format, holiday-promo, local-food, logo grids) so the batch generates a known-good control population. The 3 wildcards (T11, T9, T12) ride structurally validated MY signals (artifact format CPSQL win) and PH directional signals (howmuchisyourprofit cheap leads) — they're calculated bets, not blind ones. 32 concepts split 20 safe / 12 wildcard.
