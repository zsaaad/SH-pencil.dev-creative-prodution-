# Video Brief — PAIN-03 · "Great Food, Slow Counter"

**Premise:** The kitchen is winning; the payment line is losing. Diners love the food, then hit a queue to pay that snakes out the door — and some peel away before they ever reach the counter. StoreHub QR Order & Pay makes the line disappear. Market: Malaysia · Pillar: QR Order & Pay.

## 1. The emotional job
The viewer (an F&B owner) should feel the sting of a full, happy room being quietly undone at the last step — great reviews, packed tables, and yet walk-outs and lost sales at the moment of payment. Dramatize the **S0 problem**: paying is the bottleneck, and every minute in that line is a diner (and their bill) heading for the door. End on relief, not shame — this is fixable.

## 3. Visual direction (Tier 1 = loose beats · AI-native = numbered shot lists)

### Tier 1 — Grounded arc (original)
Run with these moods, not a locked storyboard:
- **The win:** a modern, full, buzzing restaurant at peak — great plating, happy Pan-Asian diners, staff moving with confidence. Establish that the food and room are *working*.
- **The bottleneck:** pull focus to the payment line — a queue building, a couple checking the time, someone quietly deciding it's not worth it and slipping out. A high/overhead angle to reveal the line's length can land the "out the door" idea.
- **The cost made visible (data cut):** let numbers surface on screen — wait time ticking up, diners lost — Barlow, orange/black, clean.
- **The turn:** a diner at the table scans a QR tent, taps to pay, stands and leaves relaxed — no line at all. Same room, tension gone.

Keep it authentic and documentary in tone even where stylized; the venue must read upmarket and successful throughout.

- Venue reads **upmarket & aspirational**: modern well-fitted café/restaurant, good lighting, clean fit-out, successful-looking merchant. **No** roadside mamak, kopitiam, hawker stalls, cluttered/run-down interiors, or "cash tin" aesthetics.
- Faces: **Pan-Asian / Southeast Asian only**, authentically Malaysian (hijab welcome). Never Western.
- **No competitors or third-party platforms** — no delivery-app / ride-app names, logos, icons or recognisable third-party UI. Generic fictional signage only.
- Never the word **"till"** or old cash-register framing — use "cashier", "checkout", "the counter", "POS".
- Brand: **#ff9419 orange + #2f2922 black** dominant. **Barlow** headlines, **Open Sans** body/CTA. Logo wordmark **"StoreHub"** (one word, mid-cap H).
