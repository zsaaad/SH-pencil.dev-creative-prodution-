# VALUE-01 — "Home By Six" · Video Production Brief

**Premise:** The café closes at dusk, apron on its hook, and the owner is already walking home — because the day's takings reconciled themselves live as each order rang, so closing was done before the lights went off.

**Market:** Malaysia (EN / BM / ZH) · **Pillar:** POS + Reporting & Analytics — live reconciliation / hours back · **Quadrant:** Stillwater (calm payoff)

---

## 1. The emotional job

This is the antidote to the "drowning" pain films. No stress, no queue, no clock racing against you. The single feeling to land: **calm relief — reclaimed evenings.** The owner isn't hustling; they've already won. Quiet, warm, unhurried. If a viewer exhales, we nailed it. Sell the *feeling* of walking out on time, not the software.

## 3. Suggested visual direction *(Tier 1 = mood reference · AI-native sections = numbered shot lists — execute as written)*

### Tier 1 — Grounded arc (original)

- **Dusk, winding down.** Warm café at golden hour, chairs going up, last light through the front window. Unhurried pace, soft handheld.
- **The signature prop.** A large **orange wall clock reading ~6:03** somewhere in frame — hero it or let it sit quietly, your call.
- **The apron beat.** Apron lifted off, hung neatly on its hook — the visual full-stop on the workday. No paperwork, no counting.
- **Home glowing across the street.** Through the window, a family dinner warmly lit in a home opposite. The owner walks toward it, calm, keys in hand.
- **Optional micro-cutaway.** A glance at a phone/POS screen showing the day already balanced — a half-second reassurance, then back to the walk home. Keep it light; the story is the evening, not the dashboard.

Palette: warm dusk oranges and soft blacks; brand orange lives in the clock and any end-card. SEA/Malaysian owner (hijab welcome), successful and at ease.

### Per-hook AI-native treatments *(one generative-only shot each)*

**Rules for all shots below (stated once, not repeated):** one transformation per generation, ≤8s. All motion slow — nothing whips or shakes. Clock faces landing on 6:03 = practical prop or post insert. POS screens = real-UI capture in post. All type = post overlay. Faces never warp; cut around the owner, never through her. She moves at natural speed; only objects, rooms and light behave impossibly. The café stays upmarket in every frame.

**Hook #1 — "Closed the day at 6:03."**

- **Shot 1 (0–8s) — Gen 1:** Wide, empty café at golden hour; large orange wall clock centered on the back wall. The clock and back wall hold still while the rest of the room rotates half a turn around the room's centre. When the tables come back into view, every chair is upside down on its tabletop and every surface is cleared. Camera: slow push-in toward the clock, easing to a stop with the rotation. Cut on the frame both stop — the stillness hides it.
- **Shot 2 (8–12s) — Insert:** Close-up, clock face fills frame. Minute hand glides 5:58 → 6:03 and stops; second hand stops a beat later; hold one second. Camera: locked. Post: headline fades in below the clock (y ≥ 280).
- **Shot 3 (12–15s) — End card (post):** Brand-orange card: wordmark, clock at 6:03, "From RM3.40/day · 24-hour setup · 20,000+ merchants across Southeast Asia", CTA "Book a demo". Logo y ≥ 250, text ends y ≤ 1400. One slow fade-in, then hold. Lands at exactly 15s.

**Hook #3 — "I haven't counted a cent after close in months."** *(concept: months of evenings, and the chair is always empty)*

- **Shot 1 (0–8s) — Gen 1:** Locked wide of a tidy back office: clean desk, closed cash drawer, empty chair, window left, wall calendar, potted plant. Through the window the sun sets, daylight returns, sets again — six times in eight seconds, smooth, no flicker. Each pass, one calendar page detaches and joins a pile on the floor; the plant grows visibly. The chair never moves; the drawer never opens; nobody enters. Camera: locked. Cut on the sixth sunset, at the darkest warm frame.
- **Shot 2 (8–13s) — Live action:** The owner at her family dinner table, mid-laugh, passing a dish. Lamp light matches Shot 1's final dusk frame. Camera: gentle handheld, eye level. Post: hook line lower-third (ends y ≤ 1400). The counting scene is never shown — that is the point.
- **Shot 3 (13–15s) — End card (post):** Same as Hook #1 Shot 3, 2s.

### Tier 2 — AI-native alternative (full arc)

One 15s arc: wind-down → clock → apron → walk home. Four generations + one insert. Same rules as above.

- **Shot 1 (0–4s) — Gen 1 (wind-down):** Golden hour. Every plate, glass, chair and folded apron lifts a hand's width off its surface and glides straight to its closing position — plates stacked by the sink, glasses in rows on the shelf, chairs upside down on tables — one slow left-to-right wave pacing the dusk light across the floor. Nothing circles or hovers. The owner wipes the counter, no reaction. Camera: slow lateral dolly pacing the wave. Cut as the last chair touches down.
- **Shot 2 (4–7s) — Gen 2 (the sun sets early):** Medium on the owner behind the counter, window behind her. The sun slides down and sets in three seconds while she folds a cloth; the light sweeps bright gold → deep amber in one continuous change. She glances up at the clock. Camera: slow push-in. Cut on her glance.
- **Shot 3 (7–8s) — Insert:** Clock close-up; hands settle on 6:03 and stop. Camera: locked.
- **Shot 4 (8–11s) — Gen 3 (apron):** Medium-wide. She lifts the apron over her head and lets go at chest height. It floats across the room at walking pace, holding its shape, and hangs itself on the hook. She is already walking to the door, not watching. Camera: static. Cut on the front door swinging open in the next shot.
- **Shot 5 (11–15s) — Gen 4 (walk home):** Exterior, dusk, upmarket shoplot street. She walks toward camera, unhurried, keys in hand. Behind her the café folds itself flat in four slow moves — front wall tips down, side walls fold in, roof last — each fold dimming the interior lights one step, the last light going out on the final fold. The building ends as one flat, neatly folded panel where the doorway was. She never looks back. Camera: slow dolly backward, full figure. Post: end-card elements land 14–15s per Section 4 safe zones.
- **Optional insert (between Shots 3 and 4):** Half-second POS close-up, day's total settled.

Palette and casting per Tier 1. 6s cutdown: Shot 1 + Shot 3 + Shot 5 trimmed to 2s.

## 4. Formats & duration

- **9:16** (hooks 1–3) and **1:1** (hooks 4–5), per data.
- Cut **6s and 15s** versions of each; **15s is the hero length** — enough room for the walk-out and the exhale.
- Keep all critical text (headline, logo, end-card) inside Meta safe zones: 9:16 headline `y ≥ 280`, CTA/end-card ends `y ≤ 1400`, logo `y ≥ 250`.

## 5. Must-haves — brand + compliance checklist

- ✅ Orange wall clock reading **~6:03** as a recurring signature prop.
- ✅ Warm dusk palette; brand **orange #ff9419 + black #2f2922** dominant. Barlow headlines, Open Sans body. Logo wordmark **"StoreHub"**.
- ✅ **Upmarket, well-fitted café** — good lighting, clean fit-out, successful owner. **Never** mamak / kopitiam / hawker / cluttered / run-down / "cash tin" looks.
- ✅ **Pan-Asian / SEA faces only.** Never Western.
- ✅ **No after-close cash-counting ritual.** The whole point is that it's *already done* — never show a manual counting scene as the norm. No "till" language, on-screen or in VO; use cashier / checkout / the counter / POS.
- ✅ **No competitors** — no third-party logos, app icons, or recognisable UI. Generic fictional signage only.
- ✅ Anchor facts available for end-card: **From RM3.40/day · 24-hour setup · 20,000+ merchants across Southeast Asia.**

## 6. Optional angles to explore

1. **Split-screen "then vs now"** — kept fully clean: not a grim counting scene, but simply an empty, dark back office *nobody is sitting in* anymore, versus the lit family table. Contrast by absence.
2. **Time-lapse day → the freeze at 6:03** — the day races, then everything stills the moment the clock hits 6:03 and the owner steps out.
3. **Voiceover from the family's side** — a kid/partner at the dinner table: "You're actually home." Warm, human, no product until the end-card.
