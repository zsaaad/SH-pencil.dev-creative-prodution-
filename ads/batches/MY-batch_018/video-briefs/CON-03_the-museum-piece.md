# Video Brief — CON-03 "The Museum Piece"

**Premise (one line):** In a bright modern boutique, a museum vitrine displays a paper spike, cash drawer and ledger under a brass "c. NOW" plaque — because running your shop on those today is running a museum piece. StoreHub is what everyone else already switched to.

**Market:** Malaysia · **Pillar:** scale proof / legacy contrast · **Vertical:** Retail
**Anchor facts:** From RM3.40/day · 24-hour setup · 20,000+ merchants across Southeast Asia.

---

## 1. The emotional job
Wry recognition, then a small flush of embarrassment: "wait — that's *my* setup." The vitrine reframes the merchant's own manual counter as a fossil on display. We want the viewer to laugh, then quietly not want to be the exhibit. Land it light and clever — never mocking the merchant, mocking the *old gear*. Exit on relief: modernising is easy, cheap, and 20,000+ shops already did it.

## 5. Must-haves — brand + compliance checklist
- **THE ONE RULE:** the obsolete props (paper spike, cash drawer, ledger, manual cash register) appear **ONLY sealed inside the museum vitrine** as the deliberate joke. They must **NEVER** be the shop's real, working setup. The live counter and whole boutique stay modern, bright, aspirational.
- **Venue = upmarket concept boutique.** Gallery lighting, polished floors, successful-looking. NEVER downmarket (no mamak/kopitiam/hawker/cluttered/run-down/"cash tin" looks).
- **Faces:** Pan-Asian / SEA only. Never Western. Authentically Malaysian, hijab welcome.
- **No competitors:** zero real brand names, logos or platform UI. Generic fictional signage only; "c. NOW" plaque + generic labels are fine.
- **Copy:** never use the word "till." (The physical old register may appear as a caged artifact — just don't name it a "till" on screen.)
- **Brand:** orange `#ff9419` + black `#2f2922` dominant; Barlow headlines, Open Sans body; "StoreHub" wordmark (one word, mid-cap H).
