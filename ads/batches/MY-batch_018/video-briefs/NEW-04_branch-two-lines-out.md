# NEW-04 — "Branch Two, Line's Out" · Video Production Brief

**Premise:** The owner opens a second outlet, a grand-opening queue is already out the door — and none of it fazes them, because QR Order & Pay clears the rush and outlet two was cloned from outlet one before the doors even unlocked.

**Quadrant:** Breakout (win / expansion) · **Pillar:** QR clears the opening rush · **Vertical:** F&B · **Market:** Malaysia
**Anchor facts:** From RM3.40/day · 24-hour setup · 20,000+ merchants across Southeast Asia.

---

## 1. The emotional job
This is a **flex, not a fear**. The viewer — an ambitious operator — should feel *momentum and quiet confidence*: "this is what winning looks like, and it's calmer than I expected." Growth without chaos. Opening day two, a queue down the street, and the owner is composed, in control, already scaling. Aspirational envy is the target emotion — "I want my second outlet to feel like that."

## 3. Suggested visual direction *(moods to run with — not a locked storyboard)*

*Lint note: the "street stall" WARN is inherited verbatim from the original Tier 1 beat (a compliant negation) — accepted.*

### Tier 1 — Grounded arc (original)
- **The reveal:** Open on the OUTLET 2 banner and a real queue trailing down a bright, modern high street. Energy and buzz, but upmarket — think a stylish new café/bistro launch, not a street stall.
- **The calm centre:** Find the owner amid the rush — composed, unhurried, ringing the first order on a handheld device right in the crowd. Contrast the busy queue with their ease.
- **The mechanism:** A seated diner scans a QR table tent and orders from their seat; the queue keeps moving because people aren't stuck waiting to order or pay. Show the flow, not the friction.
- **The clone (optional beat):** A quiet nod to "already set up" — the counter/POS screen lighting up looking identical to outlet one, day-one-ready.
- **Payoff frame:** Two outlets, one smooth flow. Land on brand lockup + anchor facts.

Casting & venue: Pan-Asian / SEA faces only (hijab welcome), a successful-looking owner. Good light, clean fit-out, aspirational venue throughout.

### Per-hook AI-native treatments *(one generative concept each — Veo-class)*

**Rules for all shots below (stated once, not repeated):** all type and readable screens — banner close-ups, counters, lockups, phone/POS UI — are post comps or inserts, never generated. The queue moves in every shot. Faces never warp, morph, stretch or blur — if an effect risks a face, cut wider.

**Hook #1 — "Branch two. Line's already out the door."** Concept: the whole street rotates 180° around the standing owner to reveal outlet two behind them, already busy. Two generations.

- **Shot 1 (0–2s) — Gen 1:** The owner stands mid-pavement with a coffee, facing camera. Behind them, outlet one's shopfront, queue moving inside. Camera: slow push-in.
- **Shot 2 (2–6s) — Gen 1 (the street rotates):** The entire street — buildings, pavement, queue — rotates 180 degrees around the owner like a turntable. The owner stays planted; both queues keep shuffling forward throughout. The rotation locks with outlet two behind them: new shopfront, banner overhead, its own queue moving out the door. The owner sips the coffee, no reaction. Camera: holds the owner until the lock, then pans fast to outlet two's door — cut inside the pan blur. Post: OUTLET 2 banner = post comp / track-replace (keep the pass wide if the type must read).
- **Shot 3 (6–10s) — Gen 2:** Documentary pass tracking laterally along outlet two's queue, door to street. The line never stops. A window-table diner scans the QR tent and orders seated. The owner watches from the doorway, relaxed. Post: hook line in safe zone.

**Hook #2 — "Grand opening, huge queue, and not one order dropped."** Concept: orders leave diners' phones as glowing orange orbs and fly in one unbroken line into the kitchen. Not one falls. Two generations.

- **Shot 1 (0–2s) — Gen 1:** Grand-opening queue moves along outlet two's glass front. People in the queue and at outdoor tables hold phones up, screens facing away from camera. Camera: low wide from across the street.
- **Shot 2 (2–5s) — Gen 1 (the orbs):** One glowing orange orb (`#ff9419`) lifts out of each phone. The orbs join into a single line and fly over the queue and through the open front door. The last orb lags low, skimming head height. Camera: tilts up and follows the line. Cut hides as the door frame wipes the lens.
- **Shot 3 (5–10s) — Gen 2 (the near-miss):** Interior. The orbs land on the counter in a row, each turning into a printed order ticket on touch. The lagging orb dips toward the floor — a staff member with a loaded tray crosses its path — the orb banks around the tray and lands in the row. The tray never slows; the orb never touches it. Staff pick tickets without pausing; the owner stands by the counter, arms folded, calm. Camera: steady push along the counter to the owner, holding the dip and bank in frame. Post: the instant the last orb lands, **ORDERS 214 · DROPPED 0** snaps in (Barlow, top third), ticking up.

### Tier 2 — AI-native alternative (full arc, hero 15s)

The impossible happens to buildings, streets and crowds — never the owner, who stays documentary-real and calm at the centre of every shot. Four generations, one impossible event each; every cut hidden by a stated wipe.

- **Shot 1 (0–4s) — Gen 1 (the reveal):** Static wide. The owner sips coffee on the pavement in front of an empty lot; outlet one open next door, queue moving. A complete shopfront rises straight out of the ground — glass front, lit interior, banner — its queue rising with it, already shuffling forward. The building locks level and the queue flows through the door without a pause. The owner doesn't flinch. Camera: static until the lock, then whip-pans to the front door — cut inside the blur. Post: banner = post comp.
- **Shot 2 (4–8s) — Gen 2 (the calm centre):** Inside outlet two. Everyone except the owner moves at double speed — staff, queue, trays. The owner rings the first order on a handheld at normal speed; a window-table diner scans the QR tent, no line. The speed effect lives in limbs, trays and clothing — never faces. Camera: slow orbit around the owner. Cut hides as a fast-moving staff member crosses the lens.
- **Shot 3 (8–12s) — Gen 3 (the clone):** The owner grips the counter's end and pulls it sideways; an identical counter slides out of it like a tray from a stack. Every other object follows on its own — menu boards, tables, POS terminal — each sliding out its own duplicate until a complete second interior stands beside the first, its POS already lit and configured. Camera: pulls back to hold both rooms in one frame. Cut hides as the duplicated menu board slides past the lens.
- **Shot 4 (12–15s) — Gen 4 (the payoff):** Top-down crane view. Two identical shopfronts side by side; one long queue flows up the street and splits cleanly into two moving streams, one per door. Nobody stops walking. Camera: rises straight up. Post: brand lockup (orange `#ff9419` on black `#2f2922`) comps in over the split point; **From RM3.40/day** follows.

Guardrails: venues upmarket and aspirational in every frame; signage = generic fictional OUTLET 2 only; no competitor or third-party UI anywhere, including phone and POS screens; Pan-Asian / SEA faces only.

## 5. Must-haves (brand + compliance)
- OUTLET 2 banner overhead (generic fictional signage only).
- Handheld ordering device in the owner's hands; QR table tent on a seated diner's table.
- Visible queue that keeps *moving* — the point is flow, not a stuck line.
- Composed, successful-looking Pan-Asian owner; SEA faces only, no Western casting.
- Brand: orange `#ff9419` + black `#2f2922` dominant; Barlow headlines, Open Sans body; "StoreHub" wordmark.
- Anchor fact on-screen at payoff: **From RM3.40/day**.
- **Do NOT:** name or show any competitor / delivery app / third-party UI or logo; use the word "till" or any cash-register/manual framing; show downmarket venues (mamak, kopitiam, hawker, cluttered/run-down). Say "cashier / checkout / the counter / POS".
