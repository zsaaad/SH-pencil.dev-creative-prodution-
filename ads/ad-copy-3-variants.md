## Why these 3 (sourcing notes)

- **C1 — `[No E-invoicing]` (CSV row at line 9527).** The only library row that anchors on e-Invoicing/LHDN compliance — a hard-to-fake regulatory pain that ties to multiple features (inventory, AI insights, multi-location). Caption 3 chosen because it's the "business owner" cut, broadest across F&B + retail. Also carries the 55% hardware anchor.
- **C2 — `[All Your Business Needs]` (CSV row at line 9879).** Cleanest pure all-in-one breadth message: POS + stock + staff + loyalty in one breath. Caption 2 chosen for density of features in shortest copy. Companion piece to C1.
- **C3 — `[Save Hours]` (CSV row at line 10075).** Distinct VP (time savings / staff payroll) absent from C1 + C2. The "Save 103 Hours a Year" headline is a sharp, specific claim already in the library.

**Rejected:** `[Tougher Than Ever]` (line 9973) — MS Caption 2 names Grab + Foodpanda. Cannot ship.
