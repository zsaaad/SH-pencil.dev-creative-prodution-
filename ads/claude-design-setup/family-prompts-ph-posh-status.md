# Claude Design — Family 10: PH Posh ICP, Status-Led (12 variants × 3 ratios) — NEW

**Why this family exists:** The PH jeepney price-anchor pulled cheap leads, zero SQLs — anchoring to cheap items recruited a cheap, price-sensitive self-image. This family NEVER leads with price. Every ad leads with control / status / time-freedom / identity, and is built to make an affluent, often multi-outlet, serious owner self-identify while repelling micro-vendors. Produced from a 6-round agent loop over two B2B SaaS ad inspiration galleries.

## THE DEFINING RULE (non-negotiable — every variant)
- **NO price, savings, discount, "From ₱X", "save", or "hours-saved" hook. Anywhere.** Price may appear ONLY as a tiny footnote, or be omitted entirely. Savings math is the jeepney trap — banned as a hook.
- Every ad must **self-qualify for multi-outlet / affluent / serious owners and repel tiny price-shoppers.** If a single-stall micro-vendor would feel "that's me," the execution failed.
- Lead with one of: control, status (not looking amateur), time-freedom, reliability, belonging, scaling across outlets.

## Global rules (PH market — every variant)
- Market: Philippines, natural PH English. Filipino / Pan-Asian people, authentic premium PH settings (BGC/Makati cafe, salon, clinic, boutique retail, multi-outlet F&B). Never Western.
- Colours: orange `#ff9419` + black `#2f2922` dominant; accents bold-orange `#ff630f`, azure `#2a6ee8`. **NEVER orange+green** — resolve all "live/status" dots to grey or orange, never green.
- Fonts: Barlow (headline, largest element) + Open Sans (body). Caveat for handwritten accents only.
- "StoreHub" one word, mid-cap H. Logo top-right in safe zone (orange on light, white on dark/orange).
- CTA: "Book a free demo" default; **"Contact us"** for multi-outlet/Enterprise variants (V1, V3, V7, V10).
- No competitor / 3rd-party names, logos, or app chrome (no Grab/FoodPanda/ShopeeFood/named POS). Use "delivery apps", "old POS", "manual/legacy systems".
- Feature names verbatim where shown: Multi Location Management, Reporting & Analytics, Inventory Management, Employee Management, Point of Sale.
- Meta safe zones: 1:1 = 64px · 16:9 = 100 top/150 bottom/100 side · 9:16 = 250 top/500 bottom/64 side.
- **Produce every variant at 1080×1080, 1920×1080, 1080×1920 — recompose, don't stretch.**

> TWO HARD GATES before spend: V3's shrinkage % needs a real cited benchmark (or pivot to a first-party claim); V1's dashboard needs real, signed-off figures — never a fabricated board.

> BUILD-FIRST 5: V1 (product-demo) · V2 (self-qualifying test) · V3 (risk stat) · V4 (belonging) · V5 (honest-admission). One per mechanism, so the test learns which ANGLE the posh ICP responds to. If trimming to 10, cut the two softest KEPTs: V11 (Sunday) and V12 (CPA-note).

**V1 — `watch-it-work-the-real-board`** · *product-demo* · BUILD-FIRST · *Contact us*
- Headline: "If you run 3+ outlets, this is the only screen you open on a Sunday."
- Alts: "Every outlet. One board. The actual dashboard — not a metaphor." / "Six outlets, one screen, 7am Sunday — from your kitchen table."
- Visual: The real StoreHub Multi Location Management dashboard on ONE tablet/laptop bezel (no collage), calm off-white + black `#2f2922` frame. Max 4–6 outlet tiles + one consolidated total bar = the single orange `#ff9419` focal point. Generous whitespace, anti-density. ONE tile shows a quiet caught-imperfection ("low stock — reorder sent") to convert "too-staged" skepticism into proof-of-control. Status dots grey/orange-pulse, NEVER green. No people — the screen is the hero.
- Filter: a real multi-outlet board is meaningless to a single-checkout vendor; clickers are tool-evaluators (mid/late-funnel). **Gate: real signed-off figures. Position as retargeting/late-funnel.**

**V2 — `the-number-you-cant-name`** · *self-qualifying test* · BUILD-FIRST · *Book a free demo*
- Headline: "Without checking your phone: name your worst-margin outlet — and by how many points."
- Alts: "Right now, no phone: which of your outlets is bleeding the most margin?" / "You know which outlet is winning. Do you know which one's quietly losing?"
- Visual: Premium black `#2f2922` block, single bold left-aligned Barlow headline, no people. Literalize "in your head": one faint greyed-out figure the reader expects filled in but isn't. Tiny StoreHub wordmark bottom-left; orange `#ff9419` CTA pill bottom-right. Bold-orange `#ff630f` accent only, no green.
- Filter: "which of your outlets" presupposes plural outlets + margin visibility; "by how many points" defeats the cocky bluff. "Margin points" = control metric, not savings.

**V3 — `the-shrinkage-line`** · *risk stat-hero* · BUILD-FIRST · *Contact us*

- Alts: "The leak you can't see is the one you keep paying for." / "Every outlet balanced. So why does the quarter come up short?"
- Visual: Editorial stat-hero. Huge Barlow numeral on solid black `#2f2922`, the "%" tinted bold-orange `#ff630f`. Small VISIBLE source attribution under the numeral (the citation IS the credibility). Bottom-right: a faint single Reporting & Analytics row catching the gap, lit orange. No people, almost editorial.
- Filter: no offer/discount — only a threat the qualified operator half-suspects; scale-gated by definition. **Gate: lock a citable single-vertical benchmark (don't blend F&B + retail) or pivot to a first-party claim. Claim VISIBILITY, not elimination.**
