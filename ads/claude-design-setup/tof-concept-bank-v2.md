# StoreHub TOF Concept Bank v2 — 40 thumb-stopping concepts (MY)

Rebuilt 2026-07-01 to replace the old 100-concept grid, which was AI slop: it depicted small operational pains literally, read as MOF problem-recognition (not TOF), and ~75% failed the "ownable" test. Hard evidence of the rot in the old embedded data: **"till" ×173, "kopitiam" ×22, "mamak" ×21, "cash tin" ×5** — all banned by the brand rules.

**Method:** 4 creative directors diverged (50 raw concepts across 12 psychological levers) → 1 hostile skeptic graded/deduped/cut → 1 polish pass. Every concept graded on Harry Dry's 3-rule test: 👁 visualize · ✔ falsify · 🔒 ownable. Bar = stops a cold scroll on mute in 1 sec, opens a curiosity loop, pays off with a StoreHub-**ownable** mechanism a rival can't claim.

**Coverage:** T1=4 T2=3 T3=2 T4=4 T5=4 T6=2 T7=4 T8=4 T9=4 T10=3 T11=4 T12=2. Only RM3.40/day + 20,000+ are framed as StoreHub stats; all other figures are the merchant's own scene-dressing.

Format per line: **ID · "hook"** — scrollStopper → *payoff (mechanism)* · [styleFamily]
