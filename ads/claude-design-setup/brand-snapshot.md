# StoreHub Brand Snapshot — for Claude Design

**One-line:** StoreHub is the all-in-one POS + business platform for 20,000+ F&B and retail merchants across Southeast Asia (MY, PH, TH).

---

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| StoreHub Orange | `#ff9419` | Primary brand, CTAs, hero accents |
| StoreHub Black | `#2f2922` | Text, dark backgrounds |
| Bold Orange | `#ff630f` | Accent, "bleeding" red-orange for cost cards |
| Vivid Pink | `#ff546f` | Accent CTA alternative |
| Azure Blue | `#2a6ee8` | Accent for variety |
| White | `#ffffff` | Surface |
| Warm Cream | `#fff8ea` | Soft surface (testimonials, comparison cards) |

Approved gradients (135°): Orange Warm `#ff630f → #ff9419`, Orange→Pink `#ff630f → #ff546f`, Pink→Blue `#ff546f → #2a6ee8`.

**Never:** orange + green, mixed orange+blue gradients, off-palette colours.

---

## Typography

| Level | Font | Weight | Size relative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Barlow | Black 900 | 100% (largest) |
| Sub-headline | Open Sans | SemiBold 600 | 70–75% of headline |
| Body / bullets | Open Sans | Regular 400 | 50–60% of headline |
| CTA pill | Open Sans | Bold 700 ALL CAPS | 35–45pt range |
| Display (lifestyle only) | Caveat | Regular | varies |

Headline is always the largest element. Never use same size across levels.

**Localization fonts:** Noto Sans SC (Chinese), IBM Plex Sans Thai + Noto Sans Thai (Thai), Smiley Sans (CN display), Sriracha (TH display).

---

## Logo

Wordmark: `STORE` orange + `HUB` black. H-mark logomark for icon use only.

- Light BG → orange wordmark (`logos/01-storehub-wordmark-orange-on-light.png`)
- Dark or orange BG → white reverse (`logos/02-storehub-wordmark-white-on-dark.png`)
- Mono print → black (`logos/03-storehub-wordmark-black-mono.png`)
- Icon-only contexts → H mark (`logos/04-storehub-h-mark-icon.webp`)

**Rules:** Top-right corner in ads. Never wordmark + H-mark in same frame. Never over busy photo without contrast plate. Never recolour, stretch, or rotate. Never spell as "ScoreHub" / "Store Hub".

---

## Imagery

- Pan-Asian faces only. **No Western faces.**
- Authentic Southeast Asian merchant contexts: modern cafe, modern restaurant, modern eatery, café, fine dining, fashion retail, grocery, specialty retail.
- Real food references: nasi lemak, roti canai, kopi, kaya toast, halo-halo, sisig, hot pot.
- Hardware (D3 Pro, D3 Mini, Falcon tablet) shown in real product context.
- Well-lit, sharp contrast, vibrant saturation.
- Apply 40–60 blur on busy photo backgrounds behind text.

---

## Voice

Practical · modern · empowering · authentic · upbeat. Merchant-first. Question hooks work well. Avoid corporate jargon, stock-photo cheerfulness, Western references.

**Example headlines that work:**
- "More Than Just a POS System"
- "You're paying how much in delivery fees?"
- "Your kopi. Our tech."
- "Hiring a human or a system?"
- "6 tools. Or 1."
- "Midnight. Or 30 seconds."

---

## Output formats (only 3)

- **1080×1080** — square (Meta feed primary)
- **1920×1080** — landscape (in-stream)
- **1080×1920** — vertical (Reels, Stories)

Logo always top-right within safe zone. Every static frame must include a CTA pill.

## Proven creative templates

1. **Artifact Native** — Hijack a familiar UI chrome (job post, WhatsApp chat, Google review card, bank statement, expense receipt). Highest scroll-stop in cold acquisition.
2. **Cultural Pride** — Local food/scene + heritage hook ("Your kopi. Our tech.").
3. **Competitive Contrast** — Split-screen: chaos (6 tools, manual stock count) vs StoreHub (1 tool, 30 seconds).
4. **Milestone Math** — Line-item cost card. Bleeding total in bold orange/red, "with StoreHub" row in brand orange.
5. **Testimonial** — Three templates: orange BG quote card · full-bleed photo + speech bubble · cream split (hardware + 5 stars + checklist).
