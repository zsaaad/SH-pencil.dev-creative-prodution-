# StoreHub — New Business Opening Campaign
## Pencil.dev Design Brief — Iteration 1

---

## CAMPAIGN CONTEXT

**Brand:** StoreHub — "More Than Just a POS System"
**Campaign:** New Business Opening — 55% Off POS Hardware
**Goal:** 10 ad creatives targeting businesses that just opened, renovated, or expanded
**Core insight:** These businesses spent a lot on opening/renovating and may not have capital left for a good POS system — so StoreHub is offering 55% off hardware specifically for them.
**CTA on every ad:** "Book a free demo" or "Claim Your 55% Off"

---

## BRAND DESIGN SYSTEM

### Colors
- **StoreHub Orange:** `#ff9419` — primary brand color, CTA buttons, accents
- **StoreHub Black:** `#2f2922` — text, dark backgrounds
- **Bold Orange:** `#ff630f` — accent, gradients
- **Azure Blue:** `#2a6ee8` — secondary panels (Choose Your Fighter left side)
- **White:** `#ffffff` — text on dark/orange backgrounds
- **Black 80% tint:** `#55504a` — body text on light backgrounds

### Approved Gradients
- Orange Warm: `#ff630f` → `#ff9419` at 135°
- Orange to Pink: `#ff630f` → `#ff546f` at 135°

### Typography
| Use | Font | Weight |
|-----|------|--------|
| English Headlines | Barlow | Black (900) |
| English Sub-headlines | Barlow | SemiBold (600) |
| English Body | Open Sans | Regular (400) |
| Chinese Headlines | Noto Sans SC | Bold |
| Chinese Body | Noto Sans SC | Regular |
| Thai Headlines | IBM Plex Sans Thai | Bold |
| Thai Body | Noto Sans Thai | Regular |

**Rules:**
- Headline must always be the LARGEST element — at least 30% larger than sub-headline
- Never bold every text level — reserve bold for emphasis only
- Never use same size for headline and sub-headline

### Logo Rules
- Light background → use full-color wordmark (`StoreHub Logo_Full Orange.png`)
- Dark or orange background → use white wordmark (`StoreHub_Logo_Full Positive Colour Reverse.png`)
- Always top-right corner placement
- Never use wordmark + H-mark in the same frame

### CTA Button Style
- Background: `#ff9419`
- Text: `#ffffff`, Barlow Bold
- Shape: full-width pill (border-radius ~36px)
- Height: 56–64px

### Corner Radius
- Cards and panels: 6–8px
- Badges and pill labels: fully rounded (50%)

## DESIGN RULES (non-negotiable)

1. **Single hook per ad** — never stack multiple messages in one frame
2. **Never white-background product-only** — hardware must appear in a real environment context
3. **Never dark full-frame on static ads** — confirmed conversion killer
4. **Headline always the largest element**
5. **Orange CTA pill button on every ad**
6. **Logo top-right on every ad**
7. **55% off offer must be prominent** — in headline, badge, or sub-headline
