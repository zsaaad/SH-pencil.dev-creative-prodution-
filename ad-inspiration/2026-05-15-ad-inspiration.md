## Cross-cutting patterns

- **The drive-thru is now the publicly contested QSR battleground.** Toast (April 14) and Square (May 11) are both shipping integrated menu-board-to-handoff workflows, both with named hardware partners (Delphi for Toast; Howard + Nanonation for Square), and both will demo at NRA. The two will spend the next 30 days arguing over the same operator. SEA read: drive-thru is structurally smaller in SEA, but *queue management for high-volume hawker / dabao orders* is the same problem with different surface area. A "Beep Order Queue" workflow that bridges QR ordering → kitchen display → handoff is the SEA equivalent and unclaimed.
- **NRA Show 2026 is a "from pilots to daily ops" week.** Industry preview coverage is unanimous: AI moves from experimentation to execution this week. Expect every booth to lead with a specific in-use customer story (Square will lean on Cash App Moneybot's 1M users; Toast will lean on Hungry Howie's 500-loc + Alinea + Preferred). The brand without a *named in-use customer* will look behind. Pencil.dev / StoreHub equivalent: line up 3 named SEA merchants with quotable post-Beep numbers before any major launch.
- **Fine dining and luxury hospitality is now a contested POS segment.** Toast (Alinea + Preferred Hotels) and Clover (Reserve powered by Tabit) both moved upmarket inside a 10-day window. Lightspeed's hospitality EU refocus belongs in this same bracket. Most SEA POS competitors are chasing volume-end SMBs; the fine-dining bracket in SG (Marina Bay Sands restaurants, Odette, Burnt Ends) and KL (Dewakan, Cantaloupe) is uncontested by SEA-native POS.
- **AI agents now ship with named human counterparts.** Toast IQ Grow's "Marketing Success Manager" is the new pattern — pair the AI agent with a human concierge so the operator doesn't fear handing the keys to a bot. Square's Managerbot has no equivalent human pairing yet (their Square Onboarding team is tooling-only). The AI + human hybrid is the easier sale into operators who don't trust AI on its own. SEA read: "Beep Concierge AI + a real Beep Success Manager" is the high-trust framing that beats either standalone.
- **Hardware refreshes are still in the SEA market — they're just under-reported.** HitPay shipped two new SKUs and added GrabPay + UPI rails this cycle and we caught it only because their changelog page indexes; the front-of-funnel marketing is non-existent. SoftSpace, Qashier, BIG POS, Mobi POS all have similar gaps. There is structural under-amplification of SEA POS hardware launches and the first POS to put a real social budget behind a hardware launch wins outsized SoV.
- **Brand-prestige logos are now part of the POS playbook.** Toast booked Alinea (Michelin-3-star) and Preferred Hotels in the same week the company shipped a $499 marketing product — the prestige logos are buying the credibility cover for the down-market product. SEA read: pair a Beep Standard launch with a single hawker icon (think: the most photographed Penang hawker stall) and the prestige logic transfers downward.
- **POS APIs are turning into agent platforms.** OctopusBridge's Retail Advisor on top of Lightspeed X confirms Lightspeed's API is now a third-party AI app surface. Toast's IQ Grow includes 20+ updates that are individually small but collectively API-shaped. Pencil.dev / StoreHub should think of the Beep / StoreHub API as the next agent platform in SEA — every tool a partner builds (creative agent, inventory agent, scheduling agent) is a moat against a single-vendor agent like Managerbot.
- **"AI + human" beats "AI alone" as a marketing copy frame for SMB.** Toast's $499 IQ Grow positioning ("Marketing Agent + a real human") and the prevailing SaaS data ("video testimonials beat polished video," "carousels with operator pain → solution → proof outperform agency-style creative") all point in the same direction: the SMB operator wants reassurance, not autonomy. AI-only positioning is the second-best framing of 2026.

## Creative briefs for Pencil.dev

Five concepts derived from this run's findings. None duplicate Briefs 1–5 from `2026-05-13-ad-inspiration.md`.

### Brief 1 — "Beep Order Queue" SEA-native drive-thru-equivalent (response to Toast / Square drive-thru launches)

- **Audience:** SEA F&B operators with high dabao / takeaway throughput (Klang Valley mamak, BKK food courts, SG kopitiam, Manila QSR). Specifically those who already use Beep ordering and feel the pain of the QR-scan-to-bagged-order loop.
- **Format:** A 60s vertical hero film + a 4-slide LinkedIn carousel + an in-product banner. The hero shows a Penang assam laksa stall with a queue of QR-ordered customers, a kitchen-display screen, and a bag-handoff zone — the SEA "drive-thru lane" rebuilt around mobile ordering.
- **Hook (operator-facing):** "Toast and Square just shipped drive-thru. We built the version that works without a drive-thru."
- **Visual direction:** Documentary handheld; sweat, steam, smiles; brand color overlays only in the lower third. End frame: split-screen of the Beep Order Queue UI on the operator's tablet alongside the customer's order-status screen.
- **CTA:** "Beep Order Queue — try it free for the next 14 days."
- **Differentiator vs. Toast / Square:** Their drive-thru launches assume car culture. SEA's equivalent unit is the high-throughput hawker / mamak / kopitiam queue, and the workflow they need (QR order → kitchen prep → counter handoff) is identical software with different physical surface area. First-mover claim is unclaimed.

### Brief 2 — "AI + a real human" $X/month bundle (response to Toast IQ Grow at $499/mo)

- **Audience:** SEA F&B and retail SMBs in the StoreHub / Beep funnel who are evaluating Toast IQ Grow / Square Managerbot / a local agency.
- **Format:** A landing page + 3 ad variants (LinkedIn single-image, FB / IG carousel, TikTok 30s vertical) launching a Pencil.dev / Beep "Creative Concierge" plan: AI ad-generation + a real human creative manager, single monthly price, single line of copy.
- **Hook:** "Toast charges USD$499/month for AI marketing + a human marketer. We do it for SEA-native ops, in your language, for [X price]."
- **Visual direction:** Side-by-side comparison panel — Toast IQ Grow's published $499 / English-only / US-bias positioning vs. Beep / Pencil.dev's local-language / SEA-merchant-tested / X-price positioning. Operator portrait on the right side, AI-creative-agent UI on the left.
- **CTA:** "See your first 3 ads in 24 hours. Free."
- **Differentiator vs. Toast IQ Grow:** Toast's bundle is US-only and English-only on launch. The first SEA equivalent owns the local-language and local-merchant-tested wedge. Only ship with a real human creative manager attached — the "AI + human" pattern is the full point.

### Brief 3 — "Built for Busy Hawkers" prestige-logo + down-market launch (response to Toast Alinea + Preferred + IQ Grow stack)

- **Audience:** Two simultaneously: (a) the most-photographed SEA hawker icon (e.g. Liu Sha Bao at Tian Tian, Penang Hokkien Mee at Kim Laksa) for the prestige-logo half; (b) the long tail of SEA SMB merchants for the volume half.
- **Format:** A two-part launch — a single hero piece (a 30s film + a single press hit) featuring one named hawker / kopitiam icon on Beep, paired with a $X/month "Beep Standard" tier offer that the hero piece links to. Replicates Toast's "Alinea + IQ Grow" structure: prestige logo above, mass product below.
- **Hook (hero piece):** "If [Hawker Name] runs on Beep, your stall can too."
- **Visual direction:** Single named operator portrait, lit cinematically; their actual menu board on screen; a Beep tablet visible on the counter. End frame: the Beep Standard pricing tile.
- **CTA:** "Try Beep Standard. The same software [Hawker Name] uses, RM[X]/month."
- **Differentiator vs. Toast:** Toast paired Alinea (3-Michelin) with a $499 product. SEA's equivalent prestige logos cost zero in licensing but produce more cultural earned media because they're regionally beloved. Permission, recipe royalty, and a small revenue-share are the entire cost.

### Brief 4 — "We're an agent platform, not an agent" wedge (response to OctopusBridge × Lightspeed X)

- **Audience:** SEA tech-savvy F&B and retail operators who already use 3+ tools (Beep, ShopBack, Grab, foodpanda) and want them to talk to each other. Plus SEA developers / agencies.
- **Format:** A microsite + LinkedIn thought-leadership ad series + a 60s explainer film. Position Beep / StoreHub API as an agent platform — open the door to third-party AI agents (loyalty, inventory, ad-creative) built on top of StoreHub data.
- **Hook:** "Square and Toast want to be your AI agent. We want to be the platform for *every* AI agent your business needs."
- **Visual direction:** Network-graph animation, multiple third-party logos circling the StoreHub / Beep core, individual agent UIs floating in. Soft, non-robot iconography.
- **CTA:** "Build on StoreHub. See partner agents."
- **Differentiator vs. global POS:** Square / Toast / Lightspeed are all building single, vendor-locked agents (Managerbot, Marketing Agent, Lightspeed AI). Positioning StoreHub as the *agent platform* — explicitly inviting third parties to ship agents on it — is a structurally different play that the global brands can't easily copy. Only ship after the API and a meaningful first partner are real.

### Brief 5 — "Beep Cashback × StoreHub POS" reconciliation play (response to Eats365 × ShopBack)

- **Audience:** Beep Cashback merchants + StoreHub POS merchants who currently treat cashback / loyalty as a separate spreadsheet.
- **Format:** A short product-launch announcement plus a 30s vertical and a single LinkedIn-image asset. Build the announcement around a single number — "X minutes saved per week per merchant" or "X% reduction in reconciliation errors" — once the data is real.
- **Hook:** "Stop reconciling cashback in spreadsheets. One ledger, one POS, one tap."
- **Visual direction:** A merchant's cluttered spreadsheet on the left → arrow → a clean single-screen reconciliation dashboard on the right. Restaurant-counter B-roll behind. Beep red + StoreHub yellow + a thin "ShopBack-equivalent" comparison badge.
- **CTA:** "See the new Beep Cashback × StoreHub POS view. Free for current customers."
- **Differentiator vs. Eats365 × ShopBack:** Eats365 is wiring a *third-party* rewards rail into POS. StoreHub already owns Beep Cashback — the integration is *first-party*, which means deeper data, faster rollout, no partner dependency. Tell the "we own both ends" story explicitly.
