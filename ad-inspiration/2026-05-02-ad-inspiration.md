# Competitor Ad Inspiration — 2026-05-02

## TL;DR
- Toast just dropped a major brand platform, "Built for Busy" (Arnold, April 2026), reframing "busy" as a badge of honour and converting NYC storefronts into living billboards.
- Block's Square is leaning fully local with "See You in the Neighborhood" — Neighbor Days pop-ups in NY/LA/ATL with creator partners like Killer Mike, Jimmy Butler and Emma Rogue.
- Clover is running a TV-led "Create Your Own Luck" spot starring Tabitha Brown as "Lady Luck" / Chief Empowerment Officer, debuted in Shark Tank slots.
- SumUp shipped a US ecosystem expansion (POS Lite + Terminal) on April 28, 2026 — the messaging is unapologetically "one device, run the whole shop", a cleaner positioning than its previous card-reader-centric ads.
- SEA POS players are leaning on community / loyalty narratives: StoreHub's TikTok membership reels, EasyStore's "Let Business Help Business", HitPay's "Buy From Her" — all built around the merchant as the hero, not the software.

## By competitor

### Toast
- **Source:** Ads of the World + Toast newsroom (Built for Busy press kit). Hero film "Busy" plus OOH "Windows to Success".
- **Region(s):** US (NYC OOH activations, national broadcast and digital).
- **Format:** 60–90s hero film + storefront OOH wraps + creator-led behind-the-scenes social cuts.
- **Hook / opening line:** Opens on a chef calmly dicing an onion in the middle of a packed, bustling kitchen. The camera pulls back through the restaurant window. Tagline: "Get Busy. Run Busy. Stay Busy."
- **Visual style:** Documentary cinematography, slow pull-back from intimate craft to chaos, warm restaurant lighting, no on-screen UI of the POS until late in the spot.
- **Value prop:** Toast is the platform that thrives in pressure — "built for, and pressure-tested in, high-stakes, fast-paced environments."
- **Link:** https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/built-for-busy and https://pos.toasttab.com/news/toast-built-for-busy-nyc-windows-to-success
- **Why it works:** Reframes a negative ("busy = overwhelmed") as the customer's badge of honour. Builds tribe identity before mentioning product.

> "For most people, busy means overwhelm. For Toast operators, it's the goal."

### Square (Block)
- **Source:** Square Press / Ads of the World — "See You in the Neighborhood" brand refresh.
- **Region(s):** US (NYC, LA, ATL), national digital + OOH.
- **Format:** TVC + neighbourhood OOH + Neighbor Days pop-up activations + creator collabs (apparel drops, sandwich collabs, vinyl pop-ups).
- **Hook / opening line:** "Thriving local businesses create thriving neighborhoods." Sellers featured by name: Killer Mike (The SWAG Shop, ATL), Emma Rogue (Rogue, NYC), Ggiata Delicatessen (LA), Jimmy Butler (BIGFACE Coffee).
- **Visual style:** Hyperlocal portraiture, real merchants on camera, branded pop-ups, walking-club routes through neighbourhoods.
- **Value prop:** Square = the operating system for the businesses that make a neighbourhood feel like home.
- **Link:** https://squareup.com/us/en/press/new-brand-neighborhood and https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/see-you-in-the-neighborhood
- **Why it works:** Shifts from "Square does X" to "your neighbourhood needs Square's customers." Earned-media halo from celebrity-owned SMBs, with creator drops as social proof.

### Clover (Fiserv)
- **Source:** Clover TV launch + Adweek + WWD coverage. Spot title: "Create Your Own Luck."
- **Region(s):** US — debuted March 21 on ABC's Shark Tank in Atlanta, Chicago, LA, NYC; rolled into national TV/streaming.
- **Format:** :30 / :60 TV spot + companion social shorts featuring Tabitha Brown as Chief Empowerment Officer.
- **Hook / opening line:** Tabitha Brown plays "Lady Luck", appearing in-frame to rescue an overwhelmed small business owner. Tagline: "Create your own luck."
- **Visual style:** Bright, warm, lightly comedic. Tabitha's signature wardrobe + voice-over. Real Clover hardware appears as the moment of "luck."
- **Value prop:** Clover is the partner that turns hustle into momentum — for SMBs serious about growth (not micro-merchants).
- **Link:** https://www.clover.com/tabitha-brown-chief-empowerment-officer and https://wwd.com/business-news/business-features/clover-tabitha-brown-tv-campaign-small-businesses-1237047908/
- **Why it works:** Borrows Tabitha Brown's audience equity (Black female entrepreneurs, faith-leaning SMBs). The "luck" reframe makes the product feel like a turning point rather than software.

### SumUp
- **Source:** GlobeNewswire press release (April 28, 2026) + SumUp YouTube + earlier "Make your business oOOoofficial" creative on Ads of the World.
- **Region(s):** US (new ecosystem launch); EU/UK (existing campaigns).
- **Format:** Product launch press push + product demo videos + previously-strong card-reader TVC.
- **Hook / opening line:** "One device. The whole business." Pillars now framed as "Run Your Business" (POS Lite, Terminal) and "Take Payments" (readers, invoicing).
- **Visual style:** Clean product photography of the SumUp Terminal, side-by-side comparisons with "fragmented, overpriced, and overly complex" competitor stacks (a quote from the launch release).
- **Value prop:** Consolidate fragmented stacks into one connected device — built for under-served US small businesses.
- **Link:** https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/28/3282762/0/en/sumup-expands-its-core-product-ecosystem-to-empower-millions-of-u-s-small-businesses.html and https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/make-your-business-ooooofficial
- **Why it works:** "All-in-one device" is the simplest pitch in the category right now. The launch is timed to ride share-of-voice from Toast/Square/Clover this same month.

## Cross-cutting patterns

### Hook patterns
- "Reframe the merchant identity" hooks dominate. Toast: "Busy is the goal." Clover: "Create your own luck." Square: "We see you in the neighborhood." All three name the merchant first, the product second.
- Specific outcome promises beat feature lists: StoreHub leads with "turn customers into loyal fans," not "loyalty programme module."
- "All-in-one device" is the single sharpest payments-side pitch (SumUp Terminal, Qashier, Toast Go).

### Visual / design patterns
- Documentary or "phone-shot UGC" cinematography, even on TV-budget spots (Toast Built for Busy intentionally feels like a friend's iPhone footage).
- Hardware-late, human-first — show the operator, then reveal the POS.
- Warm grades, restaurant tungsten, kitchen steam, neighbourhood golden-hour. Almost no brand is showing cold/blue/clinical-tech aesthetics in 2026.
- Storefront and street-level OOH (Toast NYC windows; Square Neighbor Days) is having a moment for category leaders.

### Offer & pricing patterns
- Promo credits and processing-fee rebates over flat discounts — Lightspeed's $350 processing credit (Mar–Jun 2026), TouchBistro's 45% off upfront eligible expenses, Epos Now's "save $750" ad framing.
- "Subsidised starter pack" plays in SEA: StoreHub × Visa, EasyStore "Let Business Help Business" community subsidies.

### Format trends (UGC vs. produced)
- UGC-style ads consistently beat polished creative by 30–40% CTR in the SaaS / SMB-tools category in 2026 (Shopify ad guide, AdStellar). All major incumbents are now seeding both formats: a TV hero and a stable of UGC variants.
- Spark Ads on TikTok (paid amplification of organic creator posts) showing a 2.6% conversion rate vs. 1.8% for studio-built in-feed ads — relevant to StoreHub, Slurp!, Eats365, iCHEF whose audiences live on TikTok.

### Localization notes (language, currency, regional cues)
- SEA POS brands are increasingly bilingual in the same creative (BM + EN, or Mandarin + EN). Currency cues default to RM / SGD / IDR depending on market.
- PayNow, GrabPay, FoodPanda integrations are now visual icons in SEA POS ads — visible shorthand for "fits how I already get paid here."
- Western incumbents keep their global voice but rarely customise for SEA. Real opportunity for SEA-native brands to look *more* local.

## Creative briefs for Pencil.dev

Five concepts ready for a designer to mock immediately.

### Brief 1 — "Busy is Good" (Pencil.dev's response to Toast)
- **Audience:** F&B operators in Klang Valley, Penang, Johor (SEA-first; Pencil.dev Beep + StoreHub merchants).
- **Format:** 30s vertical video for TikTok / IG Reels + 6s YouTube bumper cutdown.
- **Hook (0–3s):** "Friday 7:42pm. Two waiters down. Forty-three orders open. Busy looks like this." (Voiceover over a real kitchen pull-back, à la Toast.)
- **Visual direction:** Warm tungsten, handheld pull-back through kitchen pass, the StoreHub orange POS tablet glows on the line. End frame: order count ticks past 50.
- **CTA:** "Run Friday like the boss you are. Try StoreHub free for 14 days."

### Brief 2 — "Owner of the Week" (Pencil.dev's response to Square Neighbor Days)
- **Audience:** Indie cafés, neighbourhood retailers in MY/SG (community-driven SMBs).
- **Format:** Carousel for IG/FB + Spark-Ads-ready creator collab on TikTok.
- **Hook:** Slide 1 — "Meet Aisyah. Her café knows your latte order before you do. Here's how she does it."
- **Visual direction:** Founder portrait + 3 in-store moments + 1 "behind the screen" StoreHub dashboard tile. Each slide has a pull-quote in BM + EN.
- **CTA:** "Tag your neighbourhood favourite — we'll feature them next."

### Brief 3 — "One Tablet, Whole Shop" (Pencil.dev's response to SumUp Terminal)
- **Audience:** First-POS shoppers — solo operators, food trucks, mobile vendors.
- **Format:** 15s static-to-motion product spot — image ad first, video upgrade for retargeting.
- **Hook:** Static image: hand holding the StoreHub tablet, copy "Cashier. Inventory. Loyalty. Beep delivery. PayNet. One tablet."
- **Visual direction:** Top-down shot, single device on a kopitiam table, branded crockery in background. No people in frame for the static; UGC voiceover for the video version.
- **CTA:** "Stop juggling apps. Run the whole shop from one screen."

### Brief 4 — "Steal This" (Pencil.dev's pattern-interrupt static)
- **Audience:** Owner-operators considering a switch from Loyverse / iCHEF / Slurp!.
- **Format:** Single static image ad for FB/IG feed. Designed to be cheap to test.
- **Hook:** Headline overlay: "Steal the cashback playbook 1,200+ KL F&B owners run on Beep →" with a half-blurred screenshot of a sample loyalty rule set.
- **Visual direction:** Notion-style notepad aesthetic, one Beep tile peeking through. Nothing fancy — Marketing Examples / Surfer pattern.
- **CTA:** "Open the playbook" → lead-magnet landing page that captures email + WhatsApp.

### Brief 5 — "POS Doesn't Mean Cashier" (category-redefining manifesto)
- **Audience:** Multi-outlet retailers / chains evaluating Xilnex, EasyStore POS, Lightspeed.
- **Format:** 90s flagship film + 6 short cutdowns.
- **Hook:** Voiceover: "Everyone says POS like it just means cashier. We disagree. POS is what you reach for when something breaks at 9am. POS is how you know which dish to drop from the menu. POS is loyalty, delivery, payments, supply chain — every business decision in one place."
- **Visual direction:** Black-and-white interview portraits of 6 SEA owners, intercut with colour macro shots of the POS in action. End on a bright orange title card: "POS, redefined. By StoreHub."
- **CTA:** "See what POS can really do — book a 20-minute walkthrough."
