# Competitor Ad Inspiration — 2026-06-06

## TL;DR

- Square Managerbot hits general availability in the US in June 2026 — the AI agent is now positioned as a free, proactive co-pilot for Square's 1M+ sellers, runs on Anthropic Sonnet + OpenAI GPT under the hood, and ships with a published feature list (daily location-level performance, stock-velocity flagging, draft staff schedules, targeted marketing campaign suggestions, catalog-issue surfacing). This is the first POS-AI brand-level claim grounded in published model-provider names — Square is using "Sonnet" and "GPT" as credibility cards, not hiding the stack.
- Shopify shipped two structurally important retail moves in the same six-week window: POS Hub (countertop USB processor for peripherals, generally available March 2, 2026) replaces flaky Bluetooth as the SMB pain-killer, and POS app v11.0/11.1/11.2/11.3 cuts staff taps per transaction with a redesigned checkout. Pair that with Shopify Summer '26 Edition (150+ changes including Tap to Pay on iPhone, brand-customizable POS panels, refunds-as-store-credit, modify subscriptions in POS). Together they reframe Shopify POS from "ecommerce-first afterthought" to "in-person-first with reliable hardware."
- Toast's April 14 launch of **Toast Drive-Thru** — unified hardware (Delphi by Toast), POS-native drive-thru mode, AI voice ordering integration (Incept AI), Speed of Service reporting, advanced order confirmation screen, drive-thru KDS — is a category move no SEA POS will match this year. The hook ("drive-thru without fragmented stacks") is structurally lift-and-shift for the Malaysian DT-cafe segment (Bask Bear DT, Zus Coffee DT, Hangry stalls) where the same fragmentation pain exists. Combine with the Toast × Uber multi-year global partnership (rolling out 2026): merchants will run Uber Eats promotions and local ads directly from Toast — the first POS to package "your delivery rail's ad budget runs from your POS dashboard."
- HitPay shipped Shopee SPayLater (Malaysia) plus PromptPay / TrueMoney / LINE Pay (Singapore) in a single release. SPayLater taps a 2.9M-user Malaysia BNPL pool; one-tap activation, no code, 30-business-day window. This is the most aggressive single-release SEA payment-rail expansion of the quarter and the clearest signal that BNPL inside the existing checkout (no redirect) is the SEA structural pattern for 2026.
- Qashier added a Front Screen Carousel feature for QashierX2 / QashierXL (launched April 9, 2026) — up to 5 rotating visuals on the customer-facing screen, updated from QashierHQ in a few clicks. The dual-screen terminal is now also a free in-store advertising surface. SEA read: every all-in-one terminal with a second screen (StoreHub Beep Counter, Slurp counter, Eats365 365pay) has the same untapped ad-real-estate. Whoever ships the "your second screen is your free ad inventory" creative wins the merchant who already paid for the hardware.
- SumUp's archive "oOOOofficial" platform (Wonderhood Studios) resurfaces as the structural template for SEA SMB ads — a transaction where the operator announces "we now take cards" and the entire street ("locals, customers, even a water-fountain ornament") lets out an extended "oooooh." The campaign achieved SumUp's highest organic search lift in category history. The reframe — "the moment your business becomes official" — is directly portable to "the moment your kedai accepts DuitNow," "the moment your warung accepts QRIS," "the moment your sari-sari store accepts InstaPay." Cheap to produce, structurally novel, evergreen.

- **Link:** https://fintechnews.hk/38324/payments/shopback-eats365-pos-integration-hong-kong-july-2026/ and https://www.techedt.com/shopback-links-rewards-and-pos-systems-with-eats365-to-extend-offline-dining-payments and https://technode.global/2026/04/14/payment-platform-shopback-restaurant-tech-platform-eats365-partner-up-to-power-fb-growth-in-hong-kong/ and https://www.eats365pos.com/my/apps/eber and https://www.eats365pos.com/hk/en/apps/advocado

## Cross-cutting patterns

- **Proactive vs. reactive is the new AI-creative axis.** Square Managerbot's "without ever having to ask" reframe shifts the AI conversation from "ask your data a question" (Toast IQ, Lightspeed AI conversational assistant) to "the agent flags you before you ask." Proactive registers as a different product entirely. SEA read: a Beep ad that opens on a notification — "Friday lunch is forecast 14% short staffed. Here's a draft schedule." — is the structurally equivalent move and beats every "AI for restaurants" headline because it shows the agent doing the work, not waiting for the question.
- **The customer-facing screen is unowned ad real estate.** Qashier turned the QashierX2 second screen into a free in-store ad inventory. Shopify Summer '26's POS Hub flow + brand-customizable POS panels do the same for the operator-facing display. Every SEA all-in-one terminal with a second screen has the same play available — zero new hardware required.
- **One-release multi-rail payment expansions outperform feature-by-feature drops.** HitPay's single changelog entry shipping SPayLater MY + PromptPay + TrueMoney + LINE Pay SG generated more share-of-voice in the SEA fintech press than four separate releases would. The "every payment method your customer carries — one HitPay checkout" framing scales. SEA read: bundling Atome + GrabPay PayLater + ShopBack PayLater + DuitNow QR + PromptPay + TrueMoney inside Beep in a single release is the structurally identical move available to StoreHub.
- **POS-as-marketing-console is the next surface.** Toast × Uber lets merchants run Uber Eats promotions and local ads directly from Toast. Toast IQ Grow already runs marketing from Toast. Square Managerbot surfaces "targeted marketing campaign opportunities" without prompt. SEA read: a Beep dashboard that runs ShopeeFood + Foodpanda + Grab promos from one screen, with a "spend $X on Foodpanda this weekend, recover $Y in incremental revenue" Beep-side recommendation, is the structurally identical move.
- **Bluetooth-reliability is the most universal SMB pain point that POS marketing has not named.** Shopify shipped a $-priced object (POS Hub) and a one-line hook ("Bluetooth dropping mid-shift? We built a box for that") for it. Every SEA SMB operator has lost a sale because a card reader disconnected mid-rush. SEA read: a "we built the box your iPad-POS was missing" ad is liftable — the box doesn't even need to be the same box; the hook works on any wired vs. wireless trade-off.
- **Brand-platform sound-design devices outperform brand-platform film budgets.** SumUp's Wonderhood "oOOOofficial" delivered the highest share-of-search and organic-search lift in SumUp's category history on a one-device creative (a chorus of "ooooohs"). The same device works in any SEA market — the "moment your kedai goes official" reframe needs a single rehearsal of crowd reaction. No celebrity, no studio film budget. SEA read: the "first DuitNow / first QRIS / first InstaPay" rite-of-passage moment is the open creative slot in SEA SMB POS marketing.
- **Vertical-wedge launches keep stacking.** Toast Drive-Thru for QSR DT chains. Clover Reserve for fine dining. Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan for inventory-heavy operators. Lightspeed Tempo / Tasks / Reservations for restaurant ops. Each is a vertical claim, not a horizontal feature. SEA read: the open vertical slots are still mamak, kopitiam, halal QSR, hawker, salon, dive shop. Whoever ships the first "Beep for Mamak" vertical campaign (dedicated landing page + vertical-specific feature claims + vertical-specific operator case studies) wins the entire category.
- **OCR-AI is the single highest-leverage SMB-AI workflow win.** Lightspeed AI OCR (packing-slip scan → draft PO) and AI Menu Importer (menu photo → digital menu) are the two onboarding bottlenecks every SEA F&B operator hates. SEA read: a 30-second screen-recording of "packing slip in → POS inventory updated" or "menu photo in → Beep menu live" beats every "AI for restaurants" headline because the demo shows the time saved.

## Creative briefs for Pencil.dev

Five concepts derived from this run. None duplicates briefs from `2026-06-02-ad-inspiration.md`.

### Brief 1 — "Sebelum Awak Tanya, Beep Dah Beritahu." — Proactive-AI Managerbot mirror for SEA

- **Audience:** SEA F&B operators (MY/TH/PH) who think of business AI as "ask the dashboard a question" — and have never seen an AI surface a problem before they noticed it.
- **Format:** A 30-second vertical hero film + 4-slide LinkedIn carousel + Meta single-image variant. Cold open on an operator's phone, showing a Beep notification mid-morning: "Khamis lunch: 14% short staffed. Tap untuk draf jadual." Cut to operator's face — surprise, then nod. Cut to Beep schedule UI auto-drafted. End frame: "Beep beritahu dulu. Awak putuskan." ("Beep tells you first. You decide.")
- **Hook:** "Sebelum awak tanya, Beep dah beritahu." ("Before you ask, Beep already told you.")
- **Visual direction:** Counter-cam over an operator's shoulder. Phone notification close-up. Beep dashboard UI capture for the schedule-draft moment. StoreHub orange (#ff9419) on black (#2f2922), Barlow + Open Sans. End frame fixed line: "Beep AI. Proaktif. Bukan reaktif."
- **CTA:** "Aktifkan Beep AI. Cuba percuma 14 hari."
- **Differentiator vs. Square Managerbot:** Square shipped the proactive AI but hasn't published a lift number or named operator. StoreHub can ship the same proactive frame plus a named SEA pilot operator (Mamak Bangsar, Kopitiam Kepong, Pizza Mansion KL) plus a 30-day lift number — replicating the Toast IQ Grow credibility set Square is still missing. The "Beep beritahu dulu" Bahasa frame owns the proactive register in Malay before Square enters the market.

### Brief 2 — "Skrin Kedua Awak. Iklan Percuma Awak." — Beep Counter front-screen carousel ad

- **Audience:** SEA F&B and retail operators who own a dual-screen all-in-one terminal (Beep Counter / Slurp / Eats365 / QashierX2 / Shopify POS Hub setup) and have never thought of the customer-facing screen as ad inventory they own.
- **Format:** A 25-second vertical hero film + 4-slide LinkedIn carousel + 1080×1080 Meta image. Open on a customer-facing screen showing a transaction summary. Cut to the same screen with a rotating drink-of-the-day promo. Cut to the same screen showing a Beep Cashback offer. Voiceover: "Setiap counter Beep ada satu skrin yang awak dah bayar. Sekarang ia iklan awak." ("Every Beep counter has a screen you already paid for. Now it's your ad.") End frame on Beep Counter product shot with a glowing customer-facing screen.
- **Hook:** "Skrin kedua awak. Iklan percuma awak." ("Your second screen. Your free ad.")
- **Visual direction:** Tight close-up of the Beep Counter customer-facing screen with motion-graphic overlay showing the carousel cycling through three promo cards (lunch combo, loyalty offer, payment-method reminder). End frame: Beep Counter on a kopitiam counter at golden hour. StoreHub orange on black, Barlow + Open Sans.
- **CTA:** "Aktifkan Skrin Promosi Beep. Percuma untuk semua merchant."
- **Differentiator vs. Qashier Front Screen Carousel:** Qashier shipped the carousel as a feature article; no creative film yet. StoreHub can ship the creative first — the "free ad" reframe is structurally novel because it inverts a feature ("rotating images on the second screen") into a positioning claim ("your hardware is your ad surface"). Available immediately; works on existing Beep Counter hardware.

### Brief 3 — "Imbas Resit Bekalan. Lupakan Taip." — Lightspeed AI OCR mirror as Beep onboarding-killer

- **Audience:** SEA SMB operators in F&B and retail who hate the manual data entry step in receiving stock (typing every SKU and qty from a paper supplier slip), and the equivalent pain of typing every menu item into a new POS during onboarding.
- **Format:** A 30-second pure screen-recording + counter-cam ad. No voiceover artist. Real footage of an operator at a counter holding a paper packing slip. Operator opens Beep on tablet, taps "Imbas Resit Bekalan," points camera at the slip. Captions in plain text: "12:14 — imbas resit," "12:14 — SKU dikenali," "12:14 — qty diisi," "12:15 — draf PO siap." Subtle ambient sound — no music. End frame plain: "Beep imbas. Awak luluskan. Selesai."
- **Hook:** "Imbas resit. Lupakan taip." ("Scan the slip. Forget typing.")
- **Visual direction:** Tablet + paper-slip + operator's hand. Counter-cam single take + screen-record cuts. Open Sans captions in English with Bahasa subtitle option. End frame: "Onboarding 2 minggu? Sekarang 2 minit." ("Two-week onboarding? Now two minutes.") StoreHub orange logo bottom-right only.
- **CTA:** "Cuba Beep OCR percuma. Onboarding 2 minit."
- **Differentiator vs. Lightspeed AI OCR:** Lightspeed shipped this as a global brand feature; no SEA-specific creative yet. StoreHub running the same play in Bahasa — and pairing it with the "onboarding 2 minggu → 2 minit" promise — beats every "AI for restaurants" headline in SEA because it names a specific minute-by-minute workflow win. Available as a creative now even if the OCR module is still in build; the brief can be re-shot when the feature ships.

### Brief 4 — "Hari Kedai Awak Jadi Rasmi." — SumUp "oOOOofficial" SEA-SMB structural lift

- **Audience:** Pre-StoreHub SEA SMB operators (kedai runcit, warung, sari-sari store, mamak, hawker stalls) who have never accepted a digital payment and are sitting on the edge of the decision.
- **Format:** A 45-second hero TV-style spot + 15-second vertical cuts + radio + KL / Bangkok / Manila shophouse OOH. Open on a mamak counter at lunch rush. Customer hands cash. Operator: "DuitNow boleh ke?" Operator gestures to a brand-new Beep Counter on the corner of the counter. Customer's eyes widen. Customer scans. From a side door, three other customers let out a long sustained "oooooooooooh." A neighbor across the street pokes their head out and adds an "ooooooh." The shophouse cat raises its head. End frame: "Hari kedai awak jadi rasmi." ("The day your shop becomes official.") with the Beep wordmark.
- **Hook:** "Hari kedai awak jadi rasmi." ("The day your shop becomes official.")
- **Visual direction:** Wide-frame street-level shot of a mamak / warung / sari-sari frontage in real natural light. Sound design carries the spot — silence of the transaction → sustained crowd "ooooohs" → mild ambient music close. StoreHub orange on black, Barlow + Open Sans. End cards in Bahasa, Thai, Tagalog for the three-market cut.
- **CTA:** "Buat kedai awak rasmi. Cuba Beep percuma 14 hari."
- **Differentiator vs. SumUp UK "oOOOofficial":** SumUp owns the format in the UK (TV launch June 2021). SEA SMB POS has no equivalent brand-platform creative — every SEA POS ad still sells features, not moments. StoreHub running the structural lift in SEA — at a fraction of the SumUp Wonderhood spend — owns the "first DuitNow / first QRIS / first InstaPay" rite-of-passage moment. The creative is structurally novel for SEA, near-free to produce (single street-level shoot, chorus of "ooooohs," no celebrity), and evergreen.

### Brief 5 — "Promosi Foodpanda. Jalankan Dari Beep." — Toast × Uber POS-as-marketing-console mirror

- **Audience:** SEA F&B operators running multiple delivery rails (ShopeeFood + Foodpanda + Grab + Beep direct) who currently bounce between 4 separate dashboards to set up a weekend promo.
- **Format:** A 30-second screen-recording hero + 4-slide LinkedIn carousel + Meta single-image variant. Open on an operator's tablet with 4 browser tabs open (ShopeeFood Manager, Foodpanda Partner, Grab Merchant, Beep). Operator sighs. Cut to a single Beep dashboard with all three delivery platforms inside a "Promosi" tab. Operator builds a weekend promo, ticks ShopeeFood + Foodpanda + Grab + Beep, hits "Lancar." End frame: "Satu skrin. Empat saluran. Satu promo." ("One screen. Four channels. One promo.")
- **Hook:** "Promosi Foodpanda. Jalankan dari Beep." ("Foodpanda promos. Run from Beep.")
- **Visual direction:** Tablet screen-recording throughout. UI cut-ins showing the unified Promosi tab. End frame on a phone showing the live promo appearing in all four customer apps. StoreHub orange on black, Barlow + Open Sans.
- **CTA:** "Cuba Promosi Beep. Satu skrin untuk semua saluran."
- **Differentiator vs. Toast × Uber:** Toast shipped this for Uber Eats only (US/CA → IE → UK rollout). SEA's structurally equivalent move is ShopeeFood + Foodpanda + Grab — three rails, not one. StoreHub being the first SEA POS to ship "your delivery marketplaces' ad budget runs from Beep" beats every US POS in market. The creative angle ("one screen, four channels, one promo") is the cleanest hook in the SEA POS-as-marketing-console category and there is no competitor running this brief.
