# Competitor Ad Inspiration — 2026-05-06

## TL;DR
- Toast IQ Grow (May 5, 2026) — a $499/month "AI Marketing Agent + human Marketing Success Manager" bundle. Positions marketing as something the POS does *for* you.
- Square opened Managerbot in broader US open beta (April 28, 2026) and will demo it at NRA Show Chicago, May 16–19 — hook is "Main Street's intelligent business agent."
- Two-week category flip: brand films (Built for Busy, See You in the Neighborhood) are being followed by AI-agent product launches that say "we'll run the business for you." Pencil.dev should mirror that brand-to-automation hand-off.
- Toast's earlier Marea / Matty Matheson hidden-camera prank film is still circulating organically on TikTok months after the paid run — prank format is the year's highest-leverage creative.
- Lightspeed is consolidating to "Retail NA + Hospitality EU" (Upserve divested April 29 for up to $81M); paid-media output is slowing in favour of product surfaces (Lightspeed AI, AI Showroom).

## By competitor

### Toast — "Toast IQ Grow" (marketing-agent layer under Built for Busy)
- **Source:** Business Wire + QSR Magazine + StockTitan, May 5, 2026.
- **Region(s):** US.
- **Format:** Product launch press + product-page video on toasttab.com + companion social. Not a TVC — product-led narrative push.
- **Hook / opening line:** "Take on marketing and drive demand for operators." Sub-hook: "From spotting a lunch slowdown to launching the campaign to fix it."
- **Visual style:** Dashboard screenshots of the Marketing Agent drafting a BOGO push, paired with a human "Marketing Success Manager" portrait. Warm restaurant B-roll reused from Built for Busy.
- **Value prop:** A $499/month bundle that runs marketing for the operator — explicitly framed against "purchasing point solutions" (i.e. Mailchimp / Klaviyo / SevenRooms).
- **Link:** https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260505034143/en/Toast-Debuts-Toast-IQ-Grow-to-Take-On-Marketing-and-Drive-Demand-for-Operators
- **Why it works:** Pilot data quoted publicly — "+8% sales on average vs. similar Toast restaurants, top quartile +40% in month one." Numbers-with-proof is rare in a category that defaults to "grow your business." The Marketing Success Manager pairing turns AE-coverage into a deliverable.

## Cross-cutting patterns

- **Hooks: "we'll run X for you" replaces "we'll help you do X."** Toast IQ Grow ("take on marketing for you"), Square Managerbot ("intelligent business agent"), Lightspeed AI Showroom ("a digital showcase without ecommerce") all relieve the merchant of work rather than adding more tools.
- **Numbers-with-proof copy is returning.** Toast IQ Grow's "+8% sales on average, +40% top quartile in month one" is the freshest pricing-with-proof hook in the category.
- **Vertical-specific hooks beat generic SMB framing in SEA** — Qashier's "Modern Beauty Salon in Malaysia / Philippines" guide pair (April 13, same date) is the cleanest example.
- **AI-launch visual language has converged in two weeks** to dashboard screenshots + chat-style UI thumbnails (Square Managerbot, Toast IQ Grow, Lightspeed AI). Differentiation now has to come from the human / restaurant footage around the UI, not the UI itself.
- **Hidden-camera / prank reaction footage is a high-leverage format** — Toast's Marea film keeps generating organic reshares from Matheson's TikTok months later. Cheap to clone with regional chefs.
- **Bundle-pricing-as-anti-stack play:** Toast IQ Grow at $499/month is explicit anti-Mailchimp/Klaviyo positioning. Pencil.dev's Beep + StoreHub bundle has the same structural advantage but isn't being marketed with comparable price-with-proof framing.
- **Free open beta as the campaign itself** — Square Managerbot's "free for now" is the hook. SEA equivalent: "free during launch month" + price reveal at a SEA trade event.
- **Government-partnership trust badges** (MATRADE, Visa, MDEC) remain unique to the SEA stack and unavailable to Toast/Square/Clover — underused in StoreHub's current creative.
- **UGC-style and static image ads still outperform polished content** in SaaS SMB segments per Q2 2026 Meta and TikTok ad guides. AI-agent launches are press + dashboard demos, not TVC; brand-platform budget is reserved for Built for Busy / Neighborhood-style hero films.

The "make our name a meme" play is wide open in SEA POS, where no brand has a memorable mnemonic. https://www.madx.digital/learn/best-saas-marketing-campaigns

## Creative briefs for Pencil.dev

Five concepts ready for a designer to mock immediately. Items 1–3 are direct responses to this week's competitor moves; 4–5 expand on patterns from last report and intentionally do not duplicate Briefs 1–5 from `2026-05-02-ad-inspiration.md`.

### Brief 1 — "The Marketing Manager Who Never Sleeps" (response to Toast IQ Grow)
- **Audience:** F&B and retail merchants in MY/SG currently DIY-ing marketing on Mailchimp/IG.
- **Format:** 30s vertical video for IG Reels / TikTok + 15s static cutdown for FB feed.
- **Hook (0–3s):** "Slow Tuesdays. Quiet 3pms. The 4-day Raya hangover. Here's what runs the campaign that fixes them."
- **Visual direction:** Split-screen — left, an exhausted owner closing the laptop at 11pm; right, a Beep dashboard spinning up an automated WhatsApp + email push. End frame: graph ticking up the next morning.
- **CTA:** "Beep handles the marketing. You handle the customers. Try free for 14 days."
- **Differentiator vs. Toast:** Toast prices AI at $499/month. Pencil.dev bundles it free into the existing Beep subscription — pricing is the wedge.

### Brief 2 — "Your Free AI Co-Manager" (response to Square Managerbot)
- **Audience:** SEA multi-outlet F&B and beauty operators (2–10 outlets).
- **Format:** IG/FB carousel + LinkedIn longform + a live demo at the next RestaurantAsia / Malaysia International Halal Showcase (mirroring Square's NRA booth play).
- **Hook (slide 1):** "Square just gave US sellers a free AI manager. Here's the same thing — built for KL, Penang, JB, and Singapore."
- **Visual direction:** Dashboard mockups for schedule drafting, low-stock alerts, end-of-day cash check. Final slide: three SEA-specific cases (PayNet reconciliation, FoodPanda payout match, Hari Raya rota) Managerbot cannot do.
- **CTA:** "Book the SEA AI Co-Manager preview."
- **Differentiator vs. Square:** SEA-native operations (PayNet, FoodPanda, GrabFood, Hari Raya) are the moat.

### Brief 3 — "Without the Shopify Tax" (response to Lightspeed AI Showroom)
- **Audience:** Brick-and-mortar retailers in MY/SG who explicitly do not want to operate an ecommerce store.
- **Format:** Single static landing-page-style ad for FB / IG feed + a longer-form blog post mirroring "Selling online isn't right for everyone."
- **Hook:** "You don't need a Shopify store. You need a window. Get an auto-built digital catalog from your StoreHub inventory — no monthly Shopify fee, no stock sync to manage."
- **Visual direction:** Real KL/Penang storefront window photography, with a phone showing a clean catalogue page held up against the window — side-by-side the boutique's real storefront and its auto-generated digital one.
- **CTA:** "Turn your inventory into a digital window — RM0/month."
- **Differentiator vs. Lightspeed:** Showroom is US/CA only. Pencil.dev can ship the SEA version first.

### Brief 4 — "Hidden Camera in My Kopitiam" (response to Toast × Matty Matheson Marea prank)
- **Audience:** SEA F&B operators on TikTok / IG who follow regional chef culture.
- **Format:** 90s hidden-camera film starring a recognisable SEA chef + 30s cutdown + 6-pack of social cuts.
- **Hook (0–3s):** "We didn't tell the staff. We sent in the worst customers we could find."
- **Visual direction:** Hidden-camera setup in a real KL kopitiam during peak lunch. Plants order: "kaya toast tanpa kaya," a TikToker filming every dish, "Hokkien mee with no carbs." End frame: the StoreHub tablet calmly tracking every order. Tagline: "Built for the worst Tuesday lunch you've ever had."
- **CTA:** "Whatever your customers throw at you, StoreHub keeps up. Free 14-day trial."
- **Differentiator vs. Toast:** Cast a SEA chef who already runs on StoreHub — owner-operator credibility, not paid endorsement.

### Brief 5 — "The Beep × ___ Pages" (Zapier-pattern integration series)
- **Audience:** SEA merchants searching "GrabFood + StoreHub," "FoodPanda + Beep," "PayNet + StoreHub," "TikTok Shop + StoreHub."
- **Format:** A factory of dedicated landing pages — one per integration, each with a customer story, a setup video, and small targeted ad spend on the integration query.
- **Hook (per page):** "Already running on {Integration}? Plug it into Beep in 5 minutes."
- **Visual direction:** One reusable template — integration logos joined by a plus sign, a 60s loom-style walkthrough, a merchant testimonial photo, dashboard toggle screenshot.
- **CTA:** "Connect {Integration} to Beep — free for the first 30 days."
- **Differentiator:** Programmatic SEO — no SEA POS competitor has Zapier-style app-pair pages, and the integration queries carry meaningful MY/SG monthly volume.
