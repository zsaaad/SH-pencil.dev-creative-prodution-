### Brief 1 — "Beep Wallet Savings — 3.50% setahun. Tiada permohonan." — SEA mirror of Square High Yield Savings

- **Audience:** MY + SG + TH + PH F&B and retail operators currently using Beep POS who keep daily settlement balances of RM10K+ in Beep. Secondary: operators currently routing settlements to Maybank2u / OCBC / BPI / KBank current accounts at sub-0.5% interest.

- **Hook:** "Beep Wallet Savings. 3.50% setahun. Tiada permohonan."

The 3.50% headline number is identical; the partner-bank wrapper is the SEA structural defense against a Square SEA launch.
