## BRIEF #01 — LHDN e-Invoice Compliance Notice

**Concept ID:** `B3X_01_artifact-einvoice-noncompliance`
**Theme:** T11 Artifact Native (extends — net-new artifact type vs C_007 tax form)
**Format:** Static · `1080×1080` (feed) + `1080×1920` (Stories/Reels still)
**Tool:** Figma
**Production time:** 3–4 hours

### Why this one (Adfolio framework)
- **Hook:** the viewer sees what looks like an official LHDN portal screenshot in their feed → cognitive trip → reads it before realising it's an ad.
- **Visual:** the artifact carries the entire message. No product. No model. No "happy customer" lifestyle shot.
- **CTA:** clicks to a landing page that mirrors the artifact — first scroll repeats "your receipts may be non-compliant" with the StoreHub fix.

### The fear it taps
Every Malaysian F&B owner in 2026 has e-invoice anxiety. The LHDN rollout has been the single biggest compliance story in SMB MY for 12 months. **Compliance fear converts harder than feature pitch.**

### Visual direction (exact)

A single full-bleed mockup that looks like an **LHDN MyInvois portal screenshot** captured on desktop. Key compositional pieces (top → bottom):

1. **Header bar (~80px tall)** — Dark navy `#0a2540`. Left: a stylized crest (NOT the real LHDN seal — a simplified geometric placeholder). Right: greyed-out user pill "Logged in as: [merchant name redacted]".
2. **Page title** — "MyInvois — Submission Status" in Open Sans Semibold, dark on white.
3. **The submission row** (the centerpiece):
   - Invoice ID: `INV-2026-04-0237`
   - Date: `28 April 2026, 14:23`
   - Amount: `RM 247.50`
   - Status pill: red `#d72638`, white text **"REJECTED — non-compliant format"**
4. **Expanded error panel below** (red `#fdecea` bg, red left border):
   - Header: "Validation Error · Code E-INV-014"
   - Body (Open Sans Regular): *"Receipt format does not conform to e-Invoice schema. Manual entry detected. Re-submit using compliant POS within 14 days to avoid penalty."*
   - Sub-line: "Penalty range: RM 200 – RM 20,000 per non-compliant transaction"
5. **Lower portion of canvas (~35% bottom)** — Cream `#fff8ea` band, the only "ad" surface:
   - Headline (Barlow Black, ~110px on 1080×1080): **"Are your receipts already non-compliant?"**
   - Sub (Open Sans Semibold, 48px): "StoreHub auto-generates LHDN-compliant e-invoices. Setup in 24 hours."
   - Orange CTA pill: **BOOK A FREE DEMO NOW**
   - StoreHub logo bottom-left (brand PNG, small)
   - **Mandatory disclaimer microcopy bottom-right** (Open Sans Regular, 14px, 60% opacity): *"Illustrative — not a real MyInvois screenshot. Fines vary; consult LHDN."*

### Pre-launch gates (don't ship until cleared)
- **Legal/brand review** — Meta deceptive-content policy AND LHDN impersonation risk. The disclaimer microcopy must be on every export, legible at thumbnail. If legal says no to the LHDN reference, swap to a generic "Government Tax Portal" wordmark.
- **LP continuity** — the landing page first fold must repeat "Is your POS LHDN-compliant?" with the same red/navy palette. If the LP is generic, scrap launch — Adfolio's #1 lesson is continuity.
- **Penalty range** — confirm RM 200 – RM 20,000 is defensible against current LHDN published penalty tables. If not, replace with a hedged "subject to LHDN penalty structure."

### Net-new check vs batch 3 C_007
C_007 was a **tax-form audit** mimicking annual income-tax checkboxes — pain = "hours your team spent on reconciliation." #01 is an **e-invoice rejection portal** — pain = "your receipts won't pass compliance." Different artifact (portal vs paper form), different fear (operational time vs regulatory penalty), different audience moment (reflective vs reactive). No overlap.

## BRIEF #08 — Broker WhatsApp Pitching POS Leasing

**Concept ID:** `B3X_08_artifact-whatsapp-broker-lease`
**Theme:** T11 Artifact Native (extends — net-new script vs C_002's internal staff chat)
**Format:** Static · `1080×1080` (feed) + `1080×1920` (Stories/Reels still)
**Tool:** Figma
**Production time:** 2 hours

### Why this one (Adfolio framework)
- **Hook:** the viewer sees what looks like a chat screenshot from one of those pushy POS leasing brokers they've been blocking for months → instant recognition, instant satisfaction.
- **Visual:** the artifact does the work. The viewer is the second character in the chat — the one who replied "Tahu kawan saya pakai StoreHub RM3.40/hari je."
- **CTA:** the satisfaction of the reply is the ad. The CTA is just the receipt for that feeling.

### The fear/win it taps
Door-to-door / WhatsApp POS leasing brokers are the #1 sales-friction story in MY F&B. Owners get pitched RM12,000–25,000 lease packages over 5 years with marked-up hardware and lock-in. They tell each other about the bad ones. **This ad is the "I escaped" story.**

### Visual direction (exact)

A vertical WhatsApp chat screenshot mockup. Stylize but stay 95% native — viewers should read 3 messages before realising it's an ad.

**Top header bar (`#075E54`):**
- Left chevron
- Generic round avatar (NOT a real broker's photo — use a placeholder with initials "AB")
- Contact name: **"Ah Beng — POS Trading 📞"** (fictional, generic surname; do NOT use a real company name)
- "online" status text, 12px white at 70% opacity
- Right icons: camera, phone, kebab menu

**Chat body — beige wallpaper `#ECE5DD` with the subtle doodle pattern.** Five messages in time order:

| Time | Sender | Bubble | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14:23 | Broker (incoming, white bubble) | "Boss good afternoon! Saw your new shop opening. I have special POS package for you 🎁" |
| 14:24 | Broker | "5-year lease only RM12,000. Includes hardware + software + 24/7 support 🔥" |
| 14:25 | Broker | "RM200/month je boss. Can sign today? I come over your shop now boleh ah?" |
| 14:32 | Broker | "Boss? You there?" |
| 14:47 | **You** (outgoing, `#DCF8C6` green bubble) | **"Tahu kawan saya pakai StoreHub RM3.40 sehari je. Tak payah lah bro 🙏"** |

Final message has **two blue read-receipt ticks** — the broker has read it and not replied. That silence is the punchline.

**Bottom strip outside the WhatsApp UI (cream `#fff8ea`, ~25% canvas):**
- Headline (Barlow Black, ~85px on 1080×1080): **"The maths is the maths."**
- Sub (Open Sans Semibold, 42px): "RM3.40/day. No lease. No lock-in. No mark-up."
- Orange CTA pill: **BOOK A FREE DEMO NOW**
- StoreHub logo bottom-left

**Critical chrome details (these are what sell the realism):**
- WhatsApp green `#075E54` header (not the meta-WhatsApp current update — use the classic colour merchants recognise)
- Incoming bubble: white `#FFFFFF`, 8px rounded
- Outgoing bubble: WhatsApp green `#DCF8C6`, 8px rounded
- Read receipts: blue ticks `#34B7F1`
- Timestamp inside each bubble, 11px grey
- **Do not use the WhatsApp wordmark or logo anywhere** — neutral header only
- **Do not name a real POS leasing company** — "POS Trading" is a generic deliberate fiction

## BRIEF #44 — Manglish UGC Selfie Video (15–20s vertical)

**Concept ID:** `B3X_44_ugc-manglish-selfie-vertical`
**Theme:** Net-new format — UGC video is **absent** from batch 3 entirely
**Format:** Video · `1080×1920` 9:16 (primary) + `1080×1080` 1:1 (square crop for feed)
**Tool:** iPhone + Descript / CapCut + a friendly merchant (or merchant-look actor)
**Production time:** Half a day shoot + half a day edit (assuming merchant is real)

### Why this one (Adfolio framework — adapted for video)
- **Hook:** the first 1.5 seconds are not the headline — they are the merchant's face mid-laugh in a real kopitiam. Pattern-interrupt because nothing in batch 3 was a face on a phone camera.
- **Visual:** Pan-Asian face, fluorescent kopitiam interior, phone-shot vertical, real audio noise. Looks like organic content.
- **CTA:** end card holds the StoreHub CTA but the *click* is mostly driven by the merchant's last line. Reels viewers click because the story landed, not because the button asked.

### The lever it pulls
B2B SaaS buyers in MY want "people like me." Polished produced video reads as ad. UGC reads as recommendation. **The same words said by a merchant on a phone outperform the same words said by a Pencil-generated voice-over by a multiple.**

### Casting + script

**Casting (non-negotiable):**
- Pan-Asian merchant or merchant-look actor (Chinese / Malay / Indian Malaysian — pick one and brief for authenticity)
- Visible age 35–55 (the actual buyer demographic for MY F&B POS)
- Wearing whatever they'd wear on a Tuesday morning — not styled, not pressed
- Filmed in a real or set-dressed kopitiam / mamak / cafe interior. Fluorescent overhead light, plastic chairs, a kettle visible, a real D3 POS on the counter behind them

**Script (15–20s, Manglish — read it as voice, not as written copy):**

| Time | Action | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Merchant looks at camera, mid-laugh, gestures with a kopi cup | *"Eh, I tell you something."* |
| 0:02–0:05 | Slight nod, sets the cup down | *"Before I get StoreHub, every night I count cash twice. Sometimes three times."* |
| 0:05–0:08 | Half-smile, hand on counter | *"Why? Because I never sure the till tally."* |
| 0:08–0:11 | Quick cut to POS screen showing a clean dashboard with end-of-day totals (2-second insert, no voice-over) | *(POS UI shows "Today: RM 2,347 · Reconciled ✓")* |
| 0:11–0:15 | Back to merchant, more relaxed | *"Now? Phone bunyi at 10pm. Day done. I jadi pergi tidur."* |
| 0:15–0:18 | Looks at camera | *"Try lah. RM3.40 sehari je."* |
| 0:18–0:20 | End card: cream BG, orange CTA pill | **"BOOK A FREE DEMO NOW"** + StoreHub logo |

**Captions:** Burned-in, **always on** (assume sound-off viewing). White Open Sans Bold with thin black stroke, bottom third of the frame. Captions are translated to standard English in [brackets] beneath the Manglish — e.g. *"jadi pergi tidur [I just go to sleep]"*.

### What's actually said vs what's heard
- The merchant's voice carries the entire ad.
- Use real ambient sound — kettle hiss, distant traffic, a single chair scrape. **Do not** add a music bed for the talking sections. A soft 4-bar music sting kicks in only when the end card hits.
- Two-camera setup if possible: one on the merchant, one tight on the POS screen for the 2-second insert.

### Production checklist
- [ ] Recruit one real merchant (preferred) OR cast one merchant-look actor with kopitiam background credibility. Signed talent release covering paid Meta usage in perpetuity.
- [ ] Scout / set-dress location — must read as a working MY kopitiam, not a styled cafe.
- [ ] iPhone 14 or newer, 4K 30fps, locked-off tripod for static shots; handheld for any reaction shots.
- [ ] Lavalier mic on the merchant (RØDE Wireless ME or similar) — phone mic alone will lose to fluorescent buzz.
- [ ] Real StoreHub POS on the counter showing real (or dummy) end-of-day data.
- [ ] Edit in Descript or CapCut: tight cuts (no >1s pauses), burned captions, end card 2 seconds.
- [ ] Export 9:16 (1080×1920) primary; auto-reframe 1:1 (1080×1080) for square placements.
- [ ] Soft music sting on end card only — license a track from Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or similar.

### Pre-launch gates
- **Talent release signed** before edit begins. Must cover paid Meta usage in perpetuity, no licence-renewal trap. If actor not merchant, the release must also waive any "real merchant" representation claim.
- **Manglish review** — three MY F&B owners watch the rough cut and confirm "this is what I would say." Adjust phrasing where they wince.
- **POS UI shown** — the inserted POS dashboard footage must show a believable end-of-day total and not leak any real merchant's PII. Use a dummy account.

### Net-new check vs batch 3
Batch 3 has **zero video content**. All 12 concepts are static + the 2 video-pacing concepts (C_010, C_012) were specced as statics that "could" become videos later. This brief is the only true UGC video in StoreHub's MY EN pipeline. No overlap, maximum gap-fill.

### File naming
`S1_EN_PostB3_ugc-manglish-merchant_9x16.mp4`
`S1_EN_PostB3_ugc-manglish-merchant_1x1.mp4`

### Risk + mitigation
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Casting reads as Western or generic | Cast Pan-Asian MY merchant or actor with kopitiam credibility; reject any shot with a styled/fashion-photographed face |
| Audio quality kills the punchline | Lavalier mic + real-room sound design; never voice-over a UGC ad |
| Manglish lands as parody | Three-merchant review before final cut; if any wince → rewrite |
| Music drowns the voice | Music only on end card; never under the talking |
| Hook rate too low (the first 1.5s) | A/B the first frame: mid-laugh vs holding kopi vs glancing at till. Pick the winner |

## WHY THIS IS NOT MORE PENCIL

Each of these briefs specifies:
- **Exact artifact UI references** (LHDN portal layout, WhatsApp colour codes, kopitiam interior lighting) — Pencil's library doesn't carry MY-specific chrome.
- **Manglish copy verbatim** — Pencil's English model defaults to neutral global English; the Manglish punchline is what makes #08 and #44 win.
- **Pan-Asian casting** and real-room audio — Pencil cannot direct human talent or a phone shoot.
- **Legal/brand gates** baked into the brief — Pencil produces, it does not pre-clear.
- **Continuity to the landing page** — Pencil ends at the ad; these briefs note the LP fold the ad must match.

Execute these in Figma + iPhone, not Pencil. If you want a designer hired for #01 and #08, the briefs above are exact enough that a freelancer can quote them as-is.
