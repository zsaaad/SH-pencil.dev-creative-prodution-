## TL;DR

- **Square "See You in the Neighborhood" is now Square's largest brand investment ever** — TV + streaming + OOH + radio + social + experiential across NYC, LA, Atlanta, and San Francisco, fronted by Killer Mike (The SWAG Shop, Atlanta), Emma Rogue (Rogue, NYC), Ggiata Delicatessen (LA), and Jimmy Butler (BIGFACE Coffee, SF). - **Square Moneybot (Cash App) is the proactive-AI proof point Managerbot is missing.** GA in Q1 — drew 1M active users in a single week with zero in-app or external marketing. 70% of conversations on both Managerbot and Moneybot are initiated by proactive prompts (the agent prompts the user, not the other way around). 33% of customers making a money movement through Moneybot attach to a net-new product in the process. Block's Q1 2026: gross profit $2.91B (+27% YoY), adjusted operating income $728M (+56% YoY), EPS $0.85 (+52% YoY). Jack Dorsey on the call: "I believe we evolve into an intelligence company." The proactive-AI register now has a hard adoption number — every SEA POS that's still selling "ask your data" loses the credibility war.
- **Toast IQ Grow shipped a $499/month bundle with named-operator lift numbers.** 8% average GMV lift across 144 restaurants in controlled beta. 40% lift for the top quartile. 69% higher click-through rate on Marketing Agent emails. $2,500+ average sales driven by a single "Slow Days" email. Matoi (San Diego) case: $3.4K attributed email revenue in 2 months, 4.5x ROAS, more than 3x Toast IQ Grow cost over the period. Bundled with a human Marketing Success Manager. This is the credibility set Square Managerbot still hasn't published — name the operator, name the lift, name the ROAS. SEA read: StoreHub needs a Beep Marketing Agent brief with a named SEA pilot (Mamak Bangsar / Pizza Mansion / Zus Coffee outlet) plus a 30-day lift number before Toast IQ Grow lands in the Klang Valley press.
- **Fiserv shipped Clover Reserve powered by Tabit (fine dining + hospitality, May 12 launch at NRA Show Booth 5834).** Multi-course pacing, sophisticated floor management, white-glove tableside service (open / modify / split / tip / close without breaking service), advanced coursing + pacing logic, complex menu structures, real-time reporting. It is the third vertical-wedge POS launch of the quarter (Toast Drive-Thru for QSR DT, Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan for inventory-heavy operators, Clover Reserve for fine dining). The SEA vertical slots — mamak, kopitiam, halal QSR, hawker, salon, dive shop, cafe chain — still belong to no POS player. The first SEA POS to ship "Beep for Mamak" or "Slurp for Hawker" with a vertical landing page + vertical-specific feature claims + vertical-specific operator case studies wins the entire category.
- **Sales-tax / e-invoice compliance is the SEA POS creative slot no one is running.** Fiserv embedded Avalara DAVO directly into Clover (announced April 8) — automated daily sales tax set-aside into a holding account, returns filed and remitted on the merchant's behalf, "guaranteed." Malaysia's LHDN Phase 4A (RM1M–RM5M revenue F&B) goes live January 1, 2026; Phase 5 onwards from July 2026. Loyverse Malaysia operators are paying RM45/month for JomeInvoice or MyInvoice2U bolt-ons. The open SEA brief: "Beep handle LHDN. Awak handle pelanggan." ("Beep handles LHDN. You handle customers.") A 30-second screen-recording of "Beep auto-files your LHDN consolidated B2C invoice every month" beats every "AI for restaurants" headline in MY F&B SaaS right now because it names the regulatory pain every operator is already sweating.
