## Cross-cutting patterns

- **Enterprise logos are now the primary post-earnings proof.** Toast turned its Q1 earnings beat into the Hungry Howie's announcement five days later. Square turned its Q1 beat into the Managerbot 1M-sellers reveal a day later. SEA POS competitors have not pushed comparable named-enterprise logo events at the same cadence — every named SEA chain win (e.g. a hawker collective, a coffee franchise, a regional restaurant group) is a free SoV slot.
- **AI agents have replaced "AI features" as the marketing unit.** Managerbot, Moneybot, Toast IQ Grow, Lightspeed conversational AI assistants, Faire AI brand recommendations — every major POS now sells a named agent with a persona, not a list of AI features. Beep / Pencil.dev's road map should be re-narrated against a named agent ("Beep Concierge," "Pencil Creative Director," etc.) rather than feature bullets.
- **"Manager you didn't have to hire" beats "grow your business."** Square's Moneybot framing — "the manager you didn't have to hire" / "find and fix" — is winning against Toast's "+8% lift" pricing-with-proof line on consumer empathy. Pencil.dev's positioning should test both axes (named ops outcome AND a named role replacement) rather than only the lift number.
- **The customer's storefront IS the ad.** Toast's NYC Windows to Success — turning Carmine's, Gertie, Two Hands into OOH surfaces — is the single most replicable format for a regional POS this quarter. The SEA equivalent ("turn 50 KL hawker stalls into Beep OOH for a week") is unclaimed, costs almost nothing, and produces organic content.
- **Edition / ship-list bundling now extends to leadership news.** Lightspeed's CTO appointment + 7-product ship-list (May 7) and Shopify's Editions site (150+ updates) are now the two best examples of "package multiple announcements into one cycle." SEA POS still ships announcements as singletons.
- **Date-anchored bonus-months as a rolling cadence, not a one-off.** EasyStore's May 9 → May 17 rollover proves that the bonus-months mechanic survives repetition — they refresh the deadline weekly. This is the most under-used SaaS retention pattern in SEA.
- **M&A is back as a SEA POS narrative.** 91APP × iCHEF (Nov 2025, $32M cash) and Lightspeed's Upserve divest (April 2026) are the bookends — buy the F&B POS, sell the under-performing one. This is a strategic story SEA boards and analysts will reference for the next 12 months.
- **Franchisor-attraction copy is a new POS angle.** Toast's "modernize to attract new franchisees" line in the Hungry Howie's release is the first time a POS has been openly sold as a recruiting tool for the franchisor. This re-frames POS from operations expense to franchise growth lever.
- **B2B trade-press + LinkedIn-only is leaving brand visibility on the table.** SoftSpace, Erply, Talech, Revel are all running serious payments / certification stories with almost zero consumer-facing creative. A POS with even modest paid social spend can out-share-of-voice them on every owned keyword.

### Brief 1 — "Built for Busy Mamak" OOH (response to Toast Windows to Success)

- **Format:** OOH that turns 30–50 partner restaurant windows / signboards into co-branded Beep ads, plus a 60s NYC-style hero film + 8 social cutdowns. The window vinyls double as customer-acknowledgement plaques (operator's name + "Powered by StoreHub + Beep").

- **Differentiator vs. Toast:** Toast paid for NYC OOH; the SEA equivalent costs ~1/20th and produces both organic content AND a free customer-acknowledgement program (operators love being publicly named). Only run with explicit operator consent and a small loyalty perk for participating outlets.
